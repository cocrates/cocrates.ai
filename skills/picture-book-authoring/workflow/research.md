# Optional Research (`docs/{topic}.md`)

Use this workflow when the user asks for factual or stylistic research that will feed Design (Stage ②), such as:
- historical period references
- cultural wardrobe/symbol safety
- architecture/landscape reference points
- age-appropriate vocabulary/reading level guidance

**Rule:** Research is read-only input. It must be incorporated into Design artifacts (`world-bible.md`, `characters/*.md`, `locations/*.md`, `episodes/*.md`) in Stage ②.
