# Optional Research (`docs/{topic}.md`)

Use when the user asks for factual or stylistic research that will feed Design (Stage ②), such as:

- domain accuracy for education/explainer videos
- brand/visual reference points
- music/genre references for music videos
- cultural or safety constraints for ads/shorts
- pacing references for short-form platforms

**Rule:** Research is read-only input. Incorporate findings into Design artifacts (`references.md`, catalogs, `context.md`, `sequence.md`, `segments/*.md`) in Stage ② — not only into generation prompts.
