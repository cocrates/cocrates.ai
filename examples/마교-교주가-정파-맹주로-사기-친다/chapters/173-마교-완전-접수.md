## Purpose
To process the blank 173 placeholder only as a status classification: confirm that the row is a number and nothing more, register and classify the row's current procedural status, seal only the now-existing procedural state, and close the row without receipt, approval, access, execution, content, comparison, authentication, result, responsibility, guilt, penalty, or disposition. No name, phrase, or title is read into the row; the absence of a label beside the row is recorded as absence, not as a hidden sign. The blank line must never be promoted into an event, a purge, a takeover, an identity, a claim, a cause, a route, an authorship, a responsibility, or a guilt.

## Opening Question
When a line has a number and nothing else, does the number itself tell what the line is for?

## Chapter Arc
The chapter opens with the blank unsealed 173 line beneath the sealed 172 procedural row. The line has a number and nothing else; no label, question, or date sits in the margin. Taeui has the record staff confirm that the carried line carries no content, that unsealed is a state condition rather than a sign of content, and that nothing beside the line waits to be classified; he then registers and classifies the 173 row's current procedural status only. Jin seals only the now-existing current state without authorizing access or execution; Cheongun confirms that no approved physical/custody scope exists. The three status phrases remain distinct, responsibility/guilt/penalty/final disposition remain unadjudicated, T-1 and Namgung Hwi remain held, and the next number, 174, is entered separately and left blank and unsealed.
