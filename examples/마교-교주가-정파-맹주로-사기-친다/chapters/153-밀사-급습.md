## Purpose
To formally intake the separate Chapter 153 request without allowing its wording to become authorization, custody, review, responsibility, or guilt.

## Reader Engagement (Chapter Level)
### Personal Stake
The danger is not a weapon but a record that can silently turn a request into a verdict.
### Opening Question
What will Taeui refuse to write when the blank line is being treated as an answer?
### Reader Questions
Which state is actually being recorded, and who has authority to move it to the next state?
### Stakes
A procedural shortcut could manufacture access, evidence, responsibility, and guilt where none has been established.

## Chapter Arc
Taeui receives and classifies the separate request. Jin cuts apart its implied conclusions and seals only the procedural state. Cheongun confirms that no physical/custody scope exists and therefore makes no physical report. The record closes with every substantive field preserved as blank or unadjudicated, while a distinct Chapter 154 request is planted.

#### Key Events
##### Scene 1 — 받은 것은 요청뿐
**POV:** 남궁태의 | **Location:** 감사 기록 공간/접수 책상 | **When:** Chapter 152 직후의 다음 기록 시점
**On stage:** 남궁태의, 기록 담당자
**Staging:** none

- **Situation:** 기록 담당자가 Chapter 152에서 별도로 남겨진 Chapter 153 검토 요청 초안을 접수대에 올린다.
- **Beat:** Taeui reads the wording, marks the request as received, and splits request status from authorization, access, execution, custody, review, result, responsibility, and guilt fields. He rejects wording that presupposes a completed act.
- **Turn:** The sheet is no longer a blended demand; it is formally identified as a request only, with substantive fields blank or not adjudicated.
- **Function:** Establish the chapter's central state separation and answer the prior hook without creating a result.
- **Sensory-emotional:** Dry paper catches on the record staff's fingertip -> Taeui slows his writing instead of forcing the line to close.
- **Dialogue intent:** Taeui gives exact classification instructions; the record staff repeats field labels without inference.
- **Transition out:** The separated sheet is carried to Jin for authority review, not substantive review.
- **Est. length:** 1,550 Korean characters

##### Scene 2 — 허가의 이름을 빌리지 않는 봉인
**POV:** 남궁진 | **Location:** 감사 기록 공간/접수 책상 | **When:** 같은 기록 시점
**On stage:** 남궁진, 남궁태의, 기록 담당자
**Staging:** none

- **Situation:** Jin receives the separated request and sees that blank approval lines are being read as an invitation to proceed.
- **Beat:** Jin states that he authorizes no access or execution, seals only the unapproved procedural state, and keeps substantive judgment outside the seal. Taeui accepts the narrowed wording; the record staff writes the authorization status separately.
- **Turn:** Authority is exercised as a refusal to expand scope. The seal confirms the procedural state, not content, custody, comparison, responsibility, or guilt.
- **Function:** Preserve Jin's substantive authority while preventing approval from being implied by receipt or sealing.
- **Sensory-emotional:** Wax cools before it fully glosses -> Jin leaves the judgment line untouched.
- **Dialogue intent:** Jin distinguishes permission, access, execution, and judgment in clipped declarations; Taeui confirms without arguing for a result.
- **Transition out:** The record moves to Cheongun only for a scope check; no physical/custody report is requested.
- **Est. length:** 1,650 Korean characters

##### Scene 3 — 보고할 것이 없다는 보고
**POV:** 청운 | **Location:** 감사 기록 공간/봉인 보관함 | **When:** 같은 밤
**On stage:** 청운, 남궁태의, 기록 담당자
**Staging:** none

- **Situation:** Cheongun is asked whether an approved physical/custody scope exists for the Chapter 153 row.
- **Beat:** Cheongun confirms that no approved physical/custody scope exists and therefore makes no seal, fold, wetness, pressure, surface, or custody report. The custody state is recorded as scope absent/not assessed, not as a custody fact. Taeui keeps `비교 결과 없음`, `확인 자료 없음`, and `판정 보류` in separate status fields. The record staff leaves responsibility, guilt, penalty, and final disposition blank or unadjudicated.
- **Turn:** Non-reporting and unassessed custody are preserved as boundaries, not converted into evidence, responsibility, or guilt. A separate Chapter 154 request cover is numbered and left unapproved with all substantive fields blank.
- **Function:** Close the chapter without a substantive result while planting the next procedural hook.
- **Sensory-emotional:** The empty tray makes a faint wooden knock when the new cover is placed -> Cheongun keeps his hand away from the unapproved seal line.
- **Dialogue intent:** Cheongun reports scope absence only; Taeui orders state labels kept distinct; the record staff reads back the blanks.
- **Transition out:** Two separate procedural covers remain on the desk, not as a custody finding, with no access, execution, result, responsibility, guilt, or final disposition.
- **Est. length:** 1,650 Korean characters
