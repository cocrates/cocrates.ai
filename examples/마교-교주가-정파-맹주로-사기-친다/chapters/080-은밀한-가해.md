## Chapter Arc
The opponent's weakening completes under Jegal Un's helping hand. Taeui interrupts the second contact without naming a culprit, moves the patient to the medical tent, and verifies that one peripheral arm route no longer carries force. The finding is serious but limited: the danjeon and the rest of the body are not assessed as destroyed, and recovery is unknown. In the tent, earlier records and newly reported matching symptoms create a victim pattern without a total count or public conclusion. Jegal Un remains outside the curtain, courteous and admired. Taeui chooses care and private documentation over an accusation. The closing board pairs Cheongun with Jegal Un while damaged hands tremble behind the medical screen.
