## Purpose
To process the blank 168 placeholder only as a status classification: register and classify the row's current procedural status, classify the margin question about a name as a question and not an identity finding, seal only the now-existing procedural state, and close the row without receipt, approval, access, execution, content, comparison, authentication, result, responsibility, guilt, penalty, or disposition. The title's name-question must never be promoted into a person, and the question must never pull Taeui or any other character into the record as a subject.

## Opening Question
When a blank placeholder has a question written beside it, does the question make the placeholder content?

## Chapter Arc
The chapter opens with the blank 168 line beneath the sealed 167 procedural row and an unattributed question in the margin ("정파의 남궁태의가 아니냐"). Taeui has the record staff confirm that the question is not content, that a question about a name is not a name in the record, and that identity remains `확인 자료 없음`; he then registers and classifies the 168 row's current procedural status only. Jin seals only the now-existing current state without authorizing access or execution; Cheongun confirms that no approved physical/custody scope exists. The three status phrases remain distinct, responsibility/guilt/penalty/final disposition remain unadjudicated, T-1 and Namgung Hwi remain held, and the next number, 169, is entered separately and left blank and unsealed.
