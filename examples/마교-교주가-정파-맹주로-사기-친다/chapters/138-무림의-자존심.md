# Chapter 138: 무림의 자존심

## Audit Scope
- **Authority:** `audit-serial/137-summary.md`, `audit-serial/cumulative-thread-ledger.md`, `audit-serial/cumulative-character-ledger.md` only.
- **Exclusions:** later canonical continuity, `continuity/`, and `TODO.md`.
- **Mode:** sequential audit continuation; no prior bounded result is re-decided.

## Prior Design Alignment
- **Prior hook:** 137-01 official-objection comparison sheet and 137-02 additional-original physical-comparison sheet remain separately sealed with blank result fields.
- **Picks up:** the two sealed sheets enter a formal review room.
- **Advances:** comparison scope and postponement status are recorded without producing a comparison result.
- **Plants:** a 139 formal-review docket with separate result fields and source-class verification.
- **Holds:** actual dispatch, intended receipt, handoff, transporter, authorship, responsibility, guilt, punishment, final disposition, T-1, and Namgung Hwi.

## Purpose
Make procedural delay an active choice. Taeui prepares the two sealed review sheets, Jin narrows what may be compared, and Cheongun's physical observations remain separate from claims and historical delivery questions.

## Reader Engagement
- **Opening Question:** When two sealed sheets contain different kinds of material, what may be compared without pretending the answer already exists?
- **Personal Stake:** A premature conclusion could turn a limited record into an accusation.
- **Reader Questions:** Will either sheet receive a result? Who controls the scope? What does the postponed docket require next?
- **Stakes:** procedural pressure and the risk of collapsing observation into guilt.

## Chapter Arc
The two sealed sheets are brought before one authority but never merged. Taeui reads the objection's claim and attachments separately from the additional original's physical fields. Jin rejects a demand to fill the blank result columns because the sheets were received. He confirms that receipt and physical condition prove neither dispatch, intended receipt, handoff, transporter, authorship, responsibility, guilt, nor punishment. Cheongun reports only observable condition. Formal review is postponed, and a 139 docket is sealed with both procedures independent.

## Chapter Hooks
- **Opening hook:** Two sealed sheets sit on one table, but their blank result fields do not mean the same thing.
- **Closing hook:** The 139 docket names two separate review columns and leaves both results blank.

## Exposition Budget & Seeds
- **Budget:** receipt/custody distinctions, designated fields, authority limits, and postponement terms only.
- **Seed:** Plant a sealed 139 docket for separate comparison-result review.
- **Hint:** A blank result line carries a source-class reminder rather than a conclusion.
- **Hold:** all historical delivery and responsibility questions; no T-1 or Namgung Hwi clue, action, identity, route, supplier, faction, or resolution.

## Literary Craft
- **Motifs:** two seals, blank columns, dry ink beside damp paper.
- **Reader-Discovered Meaning:** restraint is not surrender when the record is the only barrier against inventing a culprit.
- **Hold:** any declaration of innocence, guilt, or justice.
- **Closing Image:** two sealed sheets in separate sleeves beneath the new 139 docket, both result lines untouched.

## Authority and Layer Rules
- **Taeui:** receives, classifies, compares only designated fields, drafts procedure records; never declares guilt or historical delivery.
- **Jin:** approves or narrows procedure scope, seals records, retains judgment; never turns procedure into adjudication.
- **Cheongun:** reports observable seal, folds, wetness, pressure, custody, and condition only.
- **Record staff/submitter:** preserve claim, attachment, custody, physical condition, and result status as separate classes.
- **T-1 / Namgung Hwi:** no appearance, clue, action, identity, route, supplier, faction, or resolution.

### Episode 002: 판정의 연기
#### Purpose
Turn postponement into a recorded decision and establish the 139 docket.

#### Reader Engagement
- **Opening Question:** What does formal review do when material exists but required proof does not?
- **Personal Stake:** The record survives pressure only if the blank is named accurately.
- **Reader Questions:** What is postponed, and what remains authorized?
- **Stakes:** premature blame versus a usable future comparison.

#### Literary Craft
- **Motifs:** two sleeves, postponed date, ink stopping before the result line.
- **Dialogue Voices:** Taeui is precise and lightly mocking; Jin is final without omniscience; Cheongun is concrete and restrained.
- **Reader-Discovered Meaning:** postponement protects the distinction between not known and not true.
- **Hold:** guilt, innocence, authorship, route, and final disposition.

#### Exposition Budget & Seeds
- **Budget:** scope notice, postponement status, and 139 intake requirements.
- **Seed:** 139 docket with two independent review rows.
- **Hint:** one blank result line carries a source-class reminder.
- **Hold:** any result from either 137 procedure.

#### Key Events
##### Scene 1 — 연기된 판정
- **POV:** 남궁태의
- **Location:** sealed-record archive corridor / review desk / east cabinet (state: sleeves resealed)
- **When:** same formal review interval, after the scope notice
- **On stage:** 남궁태의, 남궁진, 청운, 기록 담당자
- **Staging:** archive-corridor-separate-sleeves
- **Situation:** The office must record whether either comparison has produced a result.
- **Beat:** Taeui drafts `비교 결과 없음` for both and separately retains `확인 자료 없음` and `판정 보류` fields.
- **Turn:** The 139 docket opens with independent rows while every result and responsibility field remains blank or held.
- **Function:** Close without converting procedure into guilt and provide a concrete next hook.
- **Sensory-emotional:** a brush stops before the result line -> Taeui accepts that an unfinished record can be accurate.
- **Dialogue intent:** Jin makes postponement authoritative; Taeui states what may resume; Cheongun confirms custody and condition only.
