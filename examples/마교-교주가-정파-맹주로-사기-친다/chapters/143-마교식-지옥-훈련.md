# Chapter 143: 서명 뒤의 경계

## Audit Scope
- Sequential audit source: `audit-serial/142-summary.md`, `audit-serial/cumulative-thread-ledger.md`, `audit-serial/cumulative-character-ledger.md` only.
- Later canonical continuity, `continuity/`, and `TODO.md` are excluded and remain untouched.

## Prior Design Alignment
- **Prior state:** Chapter 142 left a separate procedural-disposition request draft with approval and execution blank.
- **Revision:** The unrelated training design is removed. This chapter continues only the bounded request path.
- **Authority:** Taeui classifies and drafts; Jin authorizes only bounded access, narrows wording, seals custody, and retains substantive judgment; Cheongun reports observable condition and custody only.
- **Holds:** `비교 결과 없음`, `확인 자료 없음`, and `판정 보류` remain distinct. T-1 and Namgung Hwi remain absent and unresolved.
- **Next hook:** A separate Chapter 144 disposition-scope request is drafted with approval, access, execution, result, responsibility, and final-disposition fields blank.

## Purpose
Process the Chapter 142 procedural-disposition request without converting access authorization or evidence custody into a substantive responsibility finding.

## Reader Engagement (Chapter Level)
- **Opening Question:** A sealed request has reached its next threshold; what may a signature actually permit?
- **Personal Stake:** Taeui must keep a useful record moving without letting a procedural step become an accusation.
- **Reader Questions:** Who controls access, who keeps the material, and who is allowed to judge what it means?
- **Stakes:** One overbroad phrase could turn custody into guilt and erase the unresolved fields.

## Chapter Arc
The request is received as a request, bounded access is separately authorized, custody is separately recorded, and substantive responsibility remains held. The chapter closes on an unapproved 144 scope request.

### Episode 002: 보관의 손
#### Purpose
Record custody and observable condition without turning them into movement, authorship, or responsibility evidence.
#### Reader Engagement
- **Personal Stake:** Cheongun must report exactly what his hands can establish and no more.
- **Opening Question:** Can a packet be handled without its handling becoming a story?
- **Reader Questions:** What is observed, what is held, and what remains unknown?
- **Stakes:** Physical detail invites an inference the record must refuse.
#### Literary Craft
- **Motifs:** Sealed packet and dry ink.
- **Dialogue Voices:** Cheongun uses concrete nouns; Taeui translates no physical detail into cause; Jin cuts overclaiming wording.
- **Reader-Discovered Meaning:** Custody is a chain of responsibility for the object, not responsibility for the event.
- **Hold:** Cause, timing, movement, route, transporter, authorship.
#### Exposition Budget & Seeds
- **Budget:** Physical report limited to seal, folds, wetness, pressure, custody, surface condition.
- **Seeds:** Plant a separately numbered custody row.
- **Hold:** Authentication and comparison.
#### Key Events
##### Scene 2 — 보관대의 물성
**POV:** 남궁태의 | **Location:** 감사 기록실 | **When:** Immediately after bounded access authorization
**On stage:** 남궁태의, 남궁진, 청운, 기록 담당자
**Staging:** audit-custody-table
- **Situation:** Access to designated fields is authorized, but the packet's custody and physical report are not yet recorded.
- **Beat:** Cheongun checks the seal, folds, wetness, pressure, current custody, and surface condition; the clerk records each as a separate observable field.
- **Turn:** A pressure mark tempts a causal statement; Taeui and Jin reject it and preserve `확인 자료 없음` for dispatch, receipt, route, and transporter.
- **Function:** Separates evidence custody from substantive responsibility.
- **Sensory-emotional:** The packet's damp edge cools Cheongun's fingertips; the room grows still when he says only what he saw.
- **Dialogue intent:** Cheongun limits the report; Taeui classifies; Jin seals the boundary.
- **Transition out:** The authorized field review begins with result and comparison rows still blank.
- **Est. length:** 1,700자

### Episode 003: 판단의 빈칸
#### Purpose
Close the authorized procedural step while preserving all substantive holds and opening the next request only as a draft.
#### Reader Engagement
- **Personal Stake:** Taeui must choose whether to make the record look complete or keep its uncertainty visible.
- **Opening Question:** What can be concluded when the authorized fields contain no comparison result?
- **Reader Questions:** Will anyone fill the blank to satisfy pressure?
- **Stakes:** A completed-looking form could silently assign responsibility.
#### Literary Craft
- **Motifs:** Blank approval line, sealed packet, dry ink.
- **Dialogue Voices:** Taeui states statuses; Jin retains judgment; Cheongun answers only physical questions; clerk reads back exact wording.
- **Reader-Discovered Meaning:** An honest blank is an active procedural decision.
- **Hold:** T-1, Namgung Hwi, and all substantive responsibility.
#### Exposition Budget & Seeds
- **Budget:** No new history or lore.
- **Seeds:** Plant 144 as a separate disposition-scope request.
- **Hold:** Approval, access, execution, result, responsibility, and final-disposition fields for 144.
#### Key Events
##### Scene 3 — 빈칸의 봉인
**POV:** 남궁태의 | **Location:** 감사 기록실 | **When:** Close of the same authorized review
**On stage:** 남궁태의, 남궁진, 청운, 기록 담당자
**Staging:** audit-sealed-docket
- **Situation:** Custody is recorded, designated access is complete, and no comparison or substantive result exists.
- **Beat:** Taeui writes `비교 결과 없음`, `확인 자료 없음`, and `판정 보류` in their separate fields; Jin seals the record and refuses to convert procedure into responsibility.
- **Turn:** Taeui drafts a separate 144 disposition-scope request with every approval and execution field blank; it is placed outside the sealed 143 record.
- **Function:** Resolves the procedural step without resolving the underlying matter and plants the next hook.
- **Sensory-emotional:** The drying seal darkens while the approval line remains white; Taeui feels the weight of an unfilled space rather than relief.
- **Dialogue intent:** The three authorities distinguish access, custody, and judgment in one final read-back.
- **Transition out:** The 144 draft waits unapproved beside the sealed 143 record.
