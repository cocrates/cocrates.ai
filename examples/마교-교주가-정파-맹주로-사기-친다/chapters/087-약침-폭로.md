## Purpose
Complete the public inspection, expose the needle and matching injury evidence, and force Jegal Un's public flight without replacing the referee's final authority.

## Reader Engagement (Chapter Level)
### Personal Stake
The injured participants need the object that damaged their martial paths to be seen without turning their testimony into a mob verdict.
### Opening Question
Will the closed sleeve yield an object the arena can verify, or only another claim that Taeui staged the scene?
### Reader Questions
What does the needle prove, what remains unproven, and how far will Jegal Un run once the audience sees it?
### Stakes
Evidence credibility, participant safety, Jegal Un's public standing, and the legitimacy of the final's official record.

## Chapter Arc
The referee begins the inspection in public and records the sleeve, body, and equipment separately. The opened sleeve yields a needle bundle; Taeui compares its physical features with the injured participants' marks but refuses to name a supplier or claim that one object proves every injury. Jegal Un's polished denial breaks when his own sleeve and hand contradict it. He attempts to escape with a remaining needle, and Taeui stops him in the guest section while the referee's pending result remains for 088.
