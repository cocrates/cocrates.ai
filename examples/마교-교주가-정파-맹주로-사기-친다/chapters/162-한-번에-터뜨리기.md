# Chapter 162: 한 번에 터뜨리기

## Audit Scope
- Audit mode: sequential Chapter 162 audit only.
- Allowed authority: `audit-serial/161-summary.md`, `audit-serial/cumulative-thread-ledger.md`, `audit-serial/cumulative-character-ledger.md`.
- Later canonical continuity, `continuity/`, and `TODO.md` are excluded and unchanged.
- The charged title is a requester-provided label only. It is not a description of an event, cause, actor, or result.

## Design-First Revision
- The separate Chapter 162 draft is received and formally classified as a request during this chapter.
- Receipt and classification do not constitute approval, access, execution, custody, review, comparison, authentication, result, responsibility, guilt, penalty, or final disposition.
- The request's content and all substantive fields remain blank. No physical document, person, place, time, exchange, route, handoff, or observation is established.
- Taeui records and classifies the procedural state only. He does not inspect content or adjudicate.
- Jin seals the procedural state only. He authorizes no access or execution and retains substantive judgment.
- Cheongun confirms that no approved physical/custody scope exists and makes no physical/custody report.
- `비교 결과 없음`, `확인 자료 없음`, and `판정 보류` remain separate statuses; none is assigned as a substantive result.
- Non-receipt before intake, non-approval after intake, non-access, non-execution, and non-reporting are not converted into cause, route, authorship, responsibility, guilt, penalty, or final disposition.
- T-1 and Namgung Hwi remain absent, held, and unresolved. They do not appear, act, provide clues, acquire identities, move, or receive a resolution.
- A separate Chapter 163 draft is planted only as a numbered hook. It remains unapproved, unreceived, unexecuted, and blank in content and substantive fields.

## Purpose
Convert the unreceived Chapter 162 hook into a formally received and classified request without opening its content or creating a substantive event.

## Reader Engagement
- Opening question: Can a numbered draft become a record without becoming a fact?
- Pressure: Every procedural verb risks being mistaken for permission, access, or execution.
- Turn: Jin's seal closes the current state while leaving the request's substance untouched.
- Closing hook: A separate Chapter 163 number is left outside the record's approved scope.

## Chapter Arc
Taeui identifies the unreceived numbered hook as an intake candidate, separates its label from its substance, and records only receipt and classification. Jin narrows the record to that procedural state and seals it without authorizing access or execution. Cheongun confirms the absence of an approved physical/custody scope and provides no report. The record closes with blank result and responsibility fields, the two holds intact, and a separate unapproved Chapter 163 hook.
