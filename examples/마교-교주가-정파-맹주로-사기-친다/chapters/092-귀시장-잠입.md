## Purpose
Taeui and his merchant disguise cross the north gate and learn the minimum rules needed to stand inside the 환전 골목 without claiming control of Gu Market.
## Reader Engagement (Chapter Level)
### Personal Stake

### Opening Question

### Reader Questions

### Stakes
Entry succeeds only as a temporary tolerance; the abnormal password response marks the pair for the next inquiry.
## Chapter Arc
At the threshold Taeui performs the merchant identity prepared in 091. Inside the first lane, he and the gatekeepers establish that Gu Market uses pass edges, hand direction, black-lantern placement, and movement etiquette rather than names. Taeui follows without directing anyone, then returns one deliberately incomplete signal. The market permits the entry but answers from elsewhere, leaving 093 to determine whether the response is a password, a warning, or a copied remnant.
