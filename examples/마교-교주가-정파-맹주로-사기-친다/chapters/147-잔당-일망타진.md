## Purpose
To audit the boundary around the unapproved next review request without turning a request, custody note, or authorization into execution or judgment.

## Reader Engagement (Chapter Level)
### Personal Stake
Taeui must prevent a blank field from being filled merely because the room wants an answer; Jin must exercise authority without expanding it; Cheongun must protect the difference between what was seen and what others want it to mean.
### Opening Question
Who is allowed to write in the blank row, and what remains blank when the pressure rises?
### Reader Questions
Which field is being requested, who may authorize access, and can a carefully worded record resist an unearned conclusion?
### Stakes
The integrity of the record, the authority of the three men, and the unresolved status of the held threads.

## Chapter Arc
The chapter begins with Taeui receiving the unapproved row and refusing to treat its existence as an event. He separates the requested review from authorization and execution, then Jin narrows any possible access to designated procedural fields. Cheongun reports only observable custody and condition. The record keeper lays the fields side by side, and the chapter ends with the next row sealed as unapproved, its blanks preserved rather than solved.
