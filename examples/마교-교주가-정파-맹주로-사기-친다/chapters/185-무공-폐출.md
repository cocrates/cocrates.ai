# Chapter 185: 무공 폐출

## Audit Design Status
- This is a sequential audit design, bounded only by `audit-serial/184-summary.md`, `audit-serial/cumulative-thread-ledger.md`, and `audit-serial/cumulative-character-ledger.md`.
- Later canonical continuity is excluded. `continuity/` and `TODO.md` are not used.
- The blank Chapter 185 formal-intake placeholder is processed only as status classification. It is not a substantive event, request, receipt, result, or finding.

## Design Boundary
- The row contains the number 185 only. It is carried from no row and has no request, receipt, label, question, date, or content beside it.
- The title `무공 폐출` is a later label outside the record. It is classified as a label only and is not promoted into martial arts, expulsion, a person, an act, a cause, a judgment, guilt, responsibility, or disposition.
- Taeui records and classifies only. Jin seals only the current procedural state and authorizes no access or execution. Cheongun confirms that no approved physical/custody scope exists and makes no physical/custody report.
- `비교 결과 없음`, `확인 자료 없음`, and `판정 보류` remain distinct. No review, comparison, authentication, result, responsibility, guilt, penalty, or final disposition is produced.
- T-1 and Namgung Hwi remain absent, held, and unresolved. They do not appear, act, become clues, or receive identities, routes, factions, or resolutions.

## Purpose
Register and close the current procedural state of the blank 185 row without entering content into it.

## Reader Engagement
- **Opening Question:** What, if anything, does a number become when a blank row is classified?
- **Central tension:** The pressure to make an empty row say more than it says is refused.
- **Closing question:** What will the separately planted 186 number become, if anything?

## Chapter Arc
The 185 line is found unsealed because it has no current state. Taeui separates number from request and records the empty margin as absence. He classifies the current procedural state without opening or inventing content. Jin seals that state without authorizing access or execution. Cheongun confirms the absence of an approved physical/custody scope without reporting a physical fact. The status phrases are kept separate, the row is procedurally closed, and a separate blank 186 placeholder is planted.

## Exposition Budget & Seeds
- **Budget:** number, empty margin, classification, procedural seal, no-scope boundary, separate next placeholder.
- **Plant:** A separate 186 number only, unapproved, unreceived, unexecuted, and blank.
- **Hold:** All substantive content, comparison, authentication, result, responsibility, guilt, penalty, and final disposition.

## Literary Craft
- **Motifs:** an unmarked line, a seal applied to a state rather than content, an untouched margin.
- **Reader-Discovered Meaning:** Procedure can close a state without claiming to know what the state means.
- **Hold:** Any event or meaning suggested by the title label.
- **Closing image:** The 186 number remains separate and blank beneath the closed 185 state.
