## Purpose
Taeui removes one wasteful loop from Cheongun's third-rate sword method while proving that a first clean thrust is a repeatable beginning, not a sudden elevation in realm.

## Reader Engagement (Chapter Level)
### Personal Stake
Cheongun must make his damaged body and cheap sword reach a threat before the threat reaches the people behind him.
### Opening Question
Can a sword that is too slow still keep one person alive?
### Reader Questions
- What exactly does Taeui see in Cheongun's method that can be saved?
- Is Taeui teaching him, or merely preventing an inefficient death?
- Why does Taeui stop short of explaining the final stabbing method?
### Stakes
Cheongun's next defensive exchange, not his rank or realm, depends on correcting one recoverable motion.

## Chapter Arc
Taeui forces Cheongun to repeat the same basic cut against the worn training post, then exposes the wasted return and open waist. He physically resets only the hand, elbow, shoulder, waist, and lead foot, making clear that the correction is a line of motion rather than a new internal power. Cheongun fails because his wounded side and shallow breath remain real limits. After rest and repeated correction, he lands one centered thrust into the old split, but cannot reproduce it freely or damage the post. Taeui refuses gratitude and states that a straight line is not yet a stabbing method. He leaves the next method for 059.
