## Purpose
Use a costly, bounded confession to turn Kim Chung from captured attacker into a controlled information channel and point Chapter 009 toward prescription provenance.

## Reader Engagement (Chapter Level)
### Personal Stake
Taeui needs the truth about the poison without spending the body he is still repairing.
### Opening Question
What does Kim Chung actually know, and what did he carry without knowing?
### Reader Questions
- Is the packet connected to 혼백산, or only to Hwi's other dealings?
- Is "노 의원" a role, a name, or proof of complicity?
- Can Taeui preserve Kim Chung without trusting him?
### Stakes
Information, Kim Chung's life, Taeui's active poison and repair, and the distinction between suspicion and proof.

## Chapter Arc
Resistance becomes a narrow admission. Taeui's technique exposes stress but costs him; he stops before forcing a false certainty. Kim Chung identifies the second-assault chain's immediate handoff, not its source or result. He gives a bounded account of ledger-less goods. Taeui keeps him alive under controlled conditions and chooses documents over another round of force.

##### Scene 1 — 죽여라
**POV:** 남궁태의 | **Location:** `namgung-manor/chwuiun-gak/interior` | **When:** 007 직후, 같은 밤
