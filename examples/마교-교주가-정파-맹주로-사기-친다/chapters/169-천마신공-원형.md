## Purpose
To process the blank unsealed 169 placeholder only as a status classification: register and classify the row's current procedural status, classify the margin label about a technique as a label and not as content, a technique, a claim, or an event, seal only the now-existing procedural state, and close the row without receipt, approval, access, execution, content, comparison, authentication, result, responsibility, guilt, penalty, or disposition. The title-label must never be promoted into a technique that the record holds, never be read as anyone's knowledge or possession, and never pull any person into the record as a subject.

## Opening Question
When a blank placeholder has a name written beside it, does the name bring the thing into the record?

## Chapter Arc
The chapter opens with the blank unsealed 169 line beneath the sealed 168 procedural row and an unattributed label in the margin ("천마신공 원형"). Taeui has the record staff confirm that a label is not content, that the name of a technique is not the technique, that naming is not knowing, and that no technique, event, or person has entered the record; he then registers and classifies the 169 row's current procedural status only. Jin seals only the now-existing current state without authorizing access or execution; Cheongun confirms that no approved physical/custody scope exists. The three status phrases remain distinct, responsibility/guilt/penalty/final disposition remain unadjudicated, T-1 and Namgung Hwi remain held, and the next number, 170, is entered separately and left blank and unsealed.
