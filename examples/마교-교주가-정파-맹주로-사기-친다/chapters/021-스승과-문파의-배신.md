##### Scene 1 — 스승의 봉인
**POV:** 남궁태의 | **Location:** `namgung-manor/남궁량-처소` | **When:** 020 다음 날 아침
**On stage:** 남궁태의, 남궁혁, 남궁량, 설향
**Staging:** none
- **Situation:** Hyeok arrives while Ryang sorts the returned letters.
- **Beat:** Ryang separates the letters by seal and reads the formal refusal from Hyeok's master. The letter declines to speak for Hyeok while the records remain under review, without declaring the records true or Hyeok finally guilty. Hyeok insists the wording is temporary, Taeui points out that it avoids defending him, and Seolhyang places the unopened replies apart.
- **Turn:** Hyeok's master refuses immediate shelter, but the refusal is not a final judgment.
- **Function:** Convert the previous cliffhanger into a concrete loss.
- **Sensory-emotional:** Cold wax flakes fall onto the ledger → Taeui finds the tiny sound entertaining.
- **Dialogue intent:** Ryang states only what the documents say; Hyeok preserves dignity and keeps a procedural reply available; Taeui punctures euphemism without declaring a verdict.
- **Transition out:** Another letter arrives bearing a sect seal.

**POV:** 남궁태의 | **Location:** `namgung-manor/회랑` | **When:** 같은 날 늦은 오후

##### Scene 2 — 문밖의 요구
**POV:** 남궁태의 | **Location:** `namgung-manor/정문·외원` | **When:** 같은 날 해질녘
**On stage:** 남궁태의, 남궁혁, 설향, 이름 없는 제자·문파 관리(집단)
**Staging:** none
- **Situation:** The closed gate separates the family from the gathered group.
- **Beat:** The group demands that the records be shown and asks why Hyeok's master has withdrawn. Hyeok answers through the gate with measured courtesy, saying the matter remains under review. Taeui stops him from promising a private settlement and orders bounded copies carried to the gatehouse threshold, where they can be read without surrendering the originals or claiming a complete verdict.
- **Turn:** The evidence becomes publicly inspectable, while Hyeok's immediate support is absent; neither the crowd's final judgment nor Hyeok's complete culpability is settled.
- **Function:** End on direct social pressure and lead to Chapter 022.
- **Sensory-emotional:** Sunset catches red lacquer and trembling hands on the gate → Taeui smiles at the wood, not at Hyeok.
- **Dialogue intent:** The group demands an explanation; Hyeok offers dignity and procedural delay; Taeui permits only the bounded record to speak.
- **Transition out:** The first palm strikes the gate after Taeui's order, turning the request for explanation into Chapter 022's public-pressure hook without breaking the threshold.
- **Est. length:** ~850자
