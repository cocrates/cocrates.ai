## Design-First Revision
The previous 105 draft broke the 104 hearing handoff with monument and Gu Market material. This design keeps the entire chapter inside the hearing hall. A counter-witness is admitted as a role-bounded opposing witness, not as a final credibility answer. His cross-examination function forces both sides to identify what he directly saw, what he inferred, and which physical record can test the conflict.

## Purpose and Arc
Make the opposing testimony functional rather than decorative. The counter-witness must challenge the first witness, then be challenged on his own sight and custody. Myeongjin's martial-source attack is exposed as a separate claim whose current evidence cannot decide either witness. Jin's refusal to convert silence into assent leaves him responsible for ordering the next evidence step, which is the physical bundle handoff for Chapter 106.

1. **반대편 증언석:** Jin keeps the 104 pressure alive; the opposing witness says he saw five tied bundles leave dispatch.
2. **본 것을 심문하다:** The witness asks why his sight is discounted. Taeui separates direct sight, hearsay, tally reading, and custody; the first witness answers one question in return.
3. **봉함된 다음 질문:** Hye-gyeong presses conditional mercy, Myeongjin demands a source judgment, and Jin records both without deciding. A sealed bundle is tagged and held for 106 without opening.

## Episode Design
### Episode 001: 반대편 증언석
- **Scene 1:** The first witness remains at the seat. Myeongjin calls a dispatch-area tally clerk. The clerk saw five tied bundles at dispatch, but not the recipient, inner box count, or later ledger custody. His testimony is logged as direct sight only.
- **Scene 2:** The clerk asks why a non-martial carrier's receipt account should outweigh trained dispatch sight. Taeui answers that neither outweighs the other until time and custody align. The first witness says he saw the missing receipt and later erased name, not dispatch.

### Episode 002: 본 것을 심문하다
- **Scene 1:** Myeongjin claims an unlineaged martial artist cannot frame evidence. Taeui asks whether that proves false tally, false witness, or only non-submission. Jin records martial source separately. Hye-gyeong's mercy remains a conditional demand for submission.
- **Scene 2:** Cheongun checks attendance, receipt time, seal/tag, and custody. The counter-witness identifies a physical bundle with two red ties, a dated transfer tag, and an outer seal mark. He says it may test timing but cannot name an author. Jin forbids opening in this sitting.

### Episode 003: 봉함된 다음 질문
- **Scene 1:** Myeongjin asks Jin to declare Taeui's source illegitimate before the exhibit is opened. Taeui asks whether silence is agreement, delay, or refusal. Jin says silence is none and keeps each claim's owner visible.
- **Scene 2:** The bundle is counted, tagged, and placed into controlled custody. The tag records description, receipt time, holder, and unopened state. Jin sets the next sitting to begin with opening authority and custody. No witness is judged, no source is revealed, and no sanction is imposed.
