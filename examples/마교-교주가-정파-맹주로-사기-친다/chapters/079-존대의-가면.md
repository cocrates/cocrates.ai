## Chapter Arc
The crowd rewards Jegal Un for kneeling beside the fallen participant. Taeui separates the medical observations from the social performance and notices that the courtesy differs from Hyeok's old paternal mask: it is not a family or institutional command, but a champion's practiced access to witnesses and bodies. In the medical tent, Cheongun confirms only symptoms and remains wounded. In the corridor, Jegal Un repeats a courteous contact with the next opponent; a small loss of strength creates the Chapter 080 harm hook, while Taeui still has no complete proof.
