The invitation has been received after the public duel. It names the tournament, date, and required arrival only; it does not establish registration, a match, or a result.

### Threads This Chapter
- **Picks up:** the sealed Part 7 invitation and the public pressure surrounding Qingfeng's restored name.
- **Advances:** the tournament's public-stage promise and Taeui/Cheongun's joint arrival.
- **Plants:** the white boundary as the tournament's visible fairness claim; Jegal Un's public idol image.
- **Holds:** drug use, pressure-point destruction, victims, match results, Taeui registration, T-1, Namgung Hwi, and all later tournament outcomes.

## Purpose
Open the tournament as a public stage, place Taeui and wounded Cheongun inside it without granting new authority, and end on Jegal Un's entrance so 077 can own his substantive introduction.

## Reader Engagement (Chapter Level)
### Personal Stake
Cheongun must cross a crowd that celebrates polished orthodoxy without letting the spectacle erase the people Qingfeng still has to protect.
### Opening Question
What does Taeui notice first when the entire orthodox world gathers to applaud itself?
### Reader Questions
Why did Taeui accept the invitation's arrival requirement, and what will the crowd make of Cheongun?
### Stakes
The tournament's public credibility, Cheongun's first public exposure after Qingfeng's crisis, and the image of the champion about to enter.

## Chapter Arc
The invitation's limited terms become a physical arrival. The crowd, white boundary, rules, and staged courtesy establish the tournament's promise of fairness. Cheongun sees the appeal of the stage while Taeui measures its exits and procedures. The chapter ends before any Jegal Un match: his name is announced, the crowd turns, and the idol steps toward the white line for 077.
