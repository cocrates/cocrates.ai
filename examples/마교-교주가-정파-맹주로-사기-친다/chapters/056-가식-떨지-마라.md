### Threads This Chapter
- **Picks up:** Taeui's distrust and Cheongun's repeated care for the wounded.
- **Advances:** The physical and verbal distrust test reaches a result the reader can observe without a moral declaration.

## Purpose
Taeui tests Cheongun's face, speech, and priorities, but finds no self-serving performance to expose.
## Reader Engagement (Chapter Level)
### Personal Stake
If Taeui cannot call Cheongun's conduct a performance, his own rule that every good deed has a price loses its cleanest defense.
### Opening Question
Can a person fake sincerity when nobody is watching for praise?
### Reader Questions
What will Taeui do when the cheek test fails, and why does Cheongun continue without asking to be believed?
### Stakes
The distrust between them is tested without prematurely becoming trust, instruction, or a student bond.
## Chapter Arc
Taeui watches Cheongun return to the wounded and the entrance, attacks the supposed performance in words, and pinches his cheek to force an unguarded reaction. Cheongun shows pain and irritation but still puts the injured villagers first. Taeui tries one last bait involving reward and recognition; Cheongun refuses neither praise nor blame because neither is what he is attending to. At the close, Taeui's verbal distrust remains, but the physical evidence he wanted is absent. The unanswered conclusion becomes Chapter 057's genuine-eyes hook.
