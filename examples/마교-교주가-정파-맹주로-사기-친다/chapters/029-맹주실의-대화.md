## Purpose
Stage the first direct lord's-chamber conversation so Taeui earns a bounded inspection mandate without being made Jin's successor, then hand Chapter 030 a sealed external assignment.

## Reader Engagement (Chapter Level)
### Personal Stake
Taeui must decide whether to accept responsibility from a father who offers authority without apology, while refusing to let that authority be mistaken for office.
### Opening Question
When Jin calls him into the chamber, is Taeui being recognized, used, or tested?
### Reader Questions
What does Jin's silence protect? What can Taeui's name authorize, and what still requires Jin's seal?
### Stakes
Taeui's public credibility and the household's procedural balance.

## Chapter Arc
Inside the lord's chamber, Jin addresses Taeui as both son and accountable document-holder, but refuses emotional reconciliation. Taeui forces the exchange onto records, seals, and limits. A review of a red-sealed external report reveals an imbalance without resolving its cause. Jin grants Taeui access and a recorded inspection mandate while retaining final sealing and the power to stop the work. The chapter closes on the sealed external packet, ready to carry Taeui beyond the household in 030.
