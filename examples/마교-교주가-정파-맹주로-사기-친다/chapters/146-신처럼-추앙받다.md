## Purpose
Process the unapproved custody-scope request without converting a requested field into access, custody into responsibility, or a blank result into a conclusion.

## Reader Engagement (Chapter Level)
### Personal Stake
Taeui must stop the record from speaking beyond what anyone actually observed, while Jin must exercise authority by narrowing rather than pretending to solve the gap.
### Opening Question
If a request names a custody scope but remains unapproved, what exactly can be permitted without creating a fact?
### Reader Questions
Which field can be accessed, who may authorize it, what does Cheongun actually observe, and which blanks must survive the seal?
### Stakes
One merged line could turn procedure into accusation; one invented word could turn physical condition into responsibility.

## Chapter Arc
Taeui receives the blank request and separates its requested scope from every later procedural layer. Jin authorizes only designated observable custody fields and explicitly leaves execution, result, responsibility, and disposition outside the authorization. Cheongun reports seal, folds, wetness, pressure, surface condition, and custody without inference. Taeui and the clerk then preserve the three distinct statuses, seal the record, and create a separate unapproved next-row hook.
