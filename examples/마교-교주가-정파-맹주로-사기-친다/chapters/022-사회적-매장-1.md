## Purpose
Public anger turns Hyeok's institutional rejection into pressure the family cannot contain, without completing the final judgment reserved for Chapter 023.
## Reader Engagement (Chapter Level)
### Personal Stake
Seolhyang and the inner household are exposed to the crowd's anger.
### Opening Question
Can Hyeok still use courtesy when the crowd no longer grants him the right to explain?
### Reader Questions
Will Taeui open the gate, and what will Hyeok sacrifice to keep it shut?
### Stakes
Safety, evidence, and control of the manor's public face.
## Chapter Arc
Taeui lets the gathered people compare bounded copies while preserving the originals, verification copies, and inner household. The crowd's questions sharpen from "are these pages real?" to "why has Hyeok not answered?", but competing demands for verification keep the anger from becoming a complete social verdict. Hyeok publicly justifies temporary withdrawal as protection for the institution, yet his courteous performance cannot answer the dates. A temporary public notice is posted over his commendation; he turns toward a private appeal to Jin, planting Chapter 023's final backlash without resolving the records or his full culpability.

**Staging:** none
- **Situation:** The notice is posted beside Hyeok's old commendation.
- **Beat:** The group reads the temporary order, then notices the older commendation beneath it. Hyeok moves to remove the notice, but Taeui stops him by taking the pin from his hand. The group asks for the next document while several voices insist that a temporary suspension is not the same as a final judgment.
- **Turn:** Hyeok is physically prevented from rewriting the public wall, but the public also refuses to treat the notice as complete proof of every accusation.
- **Function:** Close on a visual reversal and propel Chapter 023.
- **Sensory-emotional:** Paste drips over the old red seal → Taeui watches the two layers sag together.
- **Dialogue intent:** Hyeok demands dignity and frames withdrawal as institutional protection; Taeui refuses removal and restates the copy/original boundary; the group demands remaining records without declaring a final verdict.
- **Transition out:** Hyeok takes the notice copy and turns from the wall toward the dark inner hall, intending a private appeal to Jin; Chapter 023 begins with that appeal and its backlash.
