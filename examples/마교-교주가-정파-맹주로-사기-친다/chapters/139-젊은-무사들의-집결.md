## Purpose
To open the two sealed procedures independently, verify only their designated fields, and postpone both results without turning procedure into judgment.

## Reader Engagement (Chapter Level)
### Personal Stake
Taeui must keep a record honest while pressure grows to make a blank column look like an answer.
### Opening Question
What can be written when the docket demands a result but the designated evidence does not authorize one?
### Reader Questions
Will the two procedures be merged? What does a physical mismatch actually permit? Who controls the blank?
### Stakes
A single overbroad phrase could turn a record into an accusation; a blank kept correctly preserves the possibility of later review.

## Chapter Arc
The office opens the two sealed sheets as separate tracks. Taeui classifies the designated fields and Cheongun reports observable condition, but neither supplies a result. Jin narrows the language, seals the blank rows, and converts pressure for a conclusion into a formal postponement and a new bounded intake hook.
