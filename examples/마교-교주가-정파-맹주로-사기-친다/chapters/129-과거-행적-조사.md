## Purpose
To receive and classify field testimony and physical traces for the two route candidates without converting any category into a route, person, responsibility, or guilt finding.

## Reader Engagement (Chapter Level)
### Personal Stake
The integrity of the inquiry depends on whether a blank can remain blank when every observer wants a name.
### Opening Question
If two observations point toward different places, can the record keep them different?
### Reader Questions
Which testimony is direct observation, and which is memory or hearsay? What do the physical marks actually show? Where will the two sheets disagree?
### Stakes
A premature merge would turn an unresolved gap into an accusation; a disciplined separation may leave the answer incomplete but usable.

## Chapter Arc
The audit office first prevents the two route candidates from being treated as one. The field testimony is received and divided by source class, then Cheongun reports only observable physical traces from separate sites and separate custody sheets. Taeui drafts a comparison-ready record without assigning a transporter or cause. Jin approves the sealed scope while explicitly keeping judgment open. The chapter closes on two records whose future comparison may expose an inconsistency, not on a solved route.
