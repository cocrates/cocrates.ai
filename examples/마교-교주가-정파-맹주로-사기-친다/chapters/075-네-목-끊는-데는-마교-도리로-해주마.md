## Purpose
Complete the procedurally accepted public duel, defeat Gojin without converting victory into a final Alliance verdict, and close Part 6 with Qingfeng’s current accusation withdrawn and a tournament invitation.

## Reader Engagement (Chapter Level)
### Personal Stake

### Opening Question
Can a duel be accepted without letting a fist replace the record?
### Reader Questions
Will Taeui honor the duel’s terms, and what exactly does Gojin lose when he falls?
### Stakes
Taeui’s public credibility, Qingfeng’s immediate safety and name, Gojin’s authority, and the small sects’ willingness to stand beside the record.

## Chapter Arc
The competent clerk converts the pending request into a personal, one-on-one challenge with public witnesses and a non-verdict clause. Gojin tries to win by orthodox sword procedure, but Taeui dismantles his timing and then disables him in a controlled sequence. The result is recorded as a duel victory, not an Alliance guilt judgment. Gojin signs a narrow correction: the current Qingfeng accusation lacks sufficient basis, the seized material and field access are to be restored pending separate review, and the evidence petition remains alive. At Qingfeng, small sects bring practical support and witness the correction. An Alliance tournament invitation pulls Taeui and Cheongun into Part 7.

**On stage:** 남궁태의, 고진, 청운, 점창파 무사들(집단), 소문파 무사들(집단)
**Staging:** none
- **Situation:** The accepted duel begins with Gojin’s sword aimed at Taeui’s throat.
- **Beat:** Taeui avoids the first line by half a step, redirects the wrist, and makes Gojin’s orthodox follow-up cuts strike empty air. Gojin escalates with his sect’s named forms; Taeui enters the lines and exposes that the authority behind the forms cannot correct their timing.
- **Turn:** The witnesses see a decisive skill gap, but no evidence claim is declared settled by the exchange.
- **Function:** Pay off the opening challenge and make the defeat earned through visible control rather than unexplained power.
- **Sensory-emotional:** Sword wind lifts a thin sheet of sand → Taeui treats the grand forms as tedious paperwork.
- **Dialogue intent:** Gojin demands respect for orthodox procedure; Taeui answers with short, cutting observations about hands and footing; Cheongun remains silent and watches the feet.
- **Transition out:** Gojin abandons measured forms and forces all remaining strength into a desperate close-range attack.

##### Scene 2 — 네 번의 소리
**POV:** 남궁태의 | **Location:** `무림맹 본산/비무장` | **When:** same afternoon
**On stage:** 남궁태의, 고진, 청운, 점창파 무사들(집단), 소문파 무사들(집단)
**Staging:** none
- **Situation:** Gojin’s desperate attack leaves four joints exposed in sequence.
- **Beat:** Taeui disables the elbow, wrist, knee, and ankle in four controlled contacts, drops Gojin without taking his life, and keeps the blade away from his throat. The clerk records surrender and physical result separately; witnesses confirm no outside intervention.
- **Turn:** Gojin loses the public qualification contest and immediate ability to continue, but the petition’s final responsibility question remains unjudged.
- **Function:** Deliver the Part 6 punishment and preserve the non-verdict boundary.
- **Sensory-emotional:** Four dry joint sounds cut through the practice yard → Cheongun does not look away, while Taeui waits for the clerk’s pen rather than the crowd’s roar.
- **Dialogue intent:** Gojin protests that defeat cannot erase procedure; Taeui says the procedure is why Gojin must now honor the terms; the clerk announces only the duel result and asks for the correction signature.
- **Transition out:** The defeated Gojin is carried to the side with temporary wraps while the signed correction form is prepared.
- **Est. length:** ~1,700자
