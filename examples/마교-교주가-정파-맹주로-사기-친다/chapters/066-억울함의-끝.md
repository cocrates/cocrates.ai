## Purpose
Cheongun is left with no institutional door to enter, while Taeui chooses a bounded Namgung family investigator mandate that will reach Qingfeng in 067.

## Reader Engagement (Chapter Level)
### Personal Stake
Cheongun has to face the people waiting behind the blockade with neither an answer nor a forceful way in.
### Opening Question
If the Alliance will not even record Qingfeng's name, what can Cheongun still protect tonight?
### Reader Questions
Will Taeui's family authority reach the medicinal field before the accusation becomes fact?
### Stakes
Cheongun's responsibility, Qingfeng's medicine and livelihood, the disputed treason evidence, and Taeui's public collision with major sect procedure.

## Chapter Arc
Cheongun returns to the blockade and sees the medicinal-field loss become a bodily shortage. He refuses violence because residents remain inside, but the refusal no longer feels like virtue; it feels like an empty hand. Taeui does not comfort or resolve him. He returns to the document room, preserves the blank as a discrepancy, and uses only the Namgung family's separate authority to issue a bounded investigator order. The chapter ends before arrival, with the rumor moving toward Qingfeng.

##### Scene 1 — 빈 칸의 주인
**POV:** 남궁태의 | **Location:** `무림맹 본산/문서실` | **When:** 066 같은 밤
**On stage:** 남궁태의, 무림맹 접수 담당자들(집단)
**Staging:** none
- **Situation:** Taeui places the three returned appeals and the copied intake ledger beside each other.
- **Beat:** He compares the repeated wording, folds, handwriting, and adjacent ledger numbers. The clerks offer jurisdiction, review, and duplicate-processing explanations. Taeui records the blank Qingfeng field and asks what separate authority can examine the missing entry.
- **Turn:** He stops waiting for the Alliance to create a case and decides to request a Namgung family direct-investigator mandate; he still does not rule on guilt or represent the Alliance.
- **Function:** Makes the authority transition procedural and bounded rather than an unexplained power-up.
- **Sensory-emotional:** Red seal dust mixes with paper dust on the desk -> Taeui smiles because the blank is more useful than the clerks' explanation, not because the case is solved.
- **Dialogue intent:** Clerks defend process and warn of jurisdiction; Taeui answers with precise reversals and requests only a lawful route.
- **Transition out:** A clerk sends the request toward the Namgung family, where the seal can be issued without changing the Alliance's absent response.

##### Scene 2 — 제한된 명령
**POV:** 남궁태의 | **Location:** `남궁세가 본가 (낙양)` | **When:** 067 새벽 직전
**On stage:** 남궁태의, 남궁세가 서리들(집단)
**Staging:** none
- **Situation:** Taeui receives the family seal box and drafts a direct-investigator order for the Qingfeng matter.
- **Beat:** He limits the scope to inspecting the medicinal-field boundary records, joint-protection documents, seized material custody, appeal intake discrepancy, and related major-school records. He lists Gojin as a responsibility subject for questioning and source comparison, not as a convicted culprit. The clerks warn that major sects will object; Taeui tells them to record the objection.
- **Turn:** The order changes Taeui's immediate status from observer to Namgung family direct investigator, while all Alliance judgment, punishment, and final responsibility remain outside the chapter.
- **Function:** Plants the exact authority needed for 067 without performing the arrival, bribe, confession, or resolution promised later.
- **Sensory-emotional:** The cold metal of the seal box presses into Taeui's wrist -> he folds the order without preparing a speech for Cheongun.
- **Dialogue intent:** Clerks state limits and risks; Taeui gives short commands that preserve source custody, objections, and separate decision authority.
- **Transition out:** The sealed order leaves with the departure party; before the chapter closes, a messenger carries the investigator's destination toward Qingfeng.
- **Est. length:** ~1,300자
