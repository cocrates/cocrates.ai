## Reader Engagement (Chapter Level)
### Personal Stake
If the petition is swallowed by the same institution that ignored Qingfeng, the public record can disappear again; if Taeui oversteps, the evidence can be recast as his private accusation.
### Opening Question
Can a man who invokes orthodox propriety force the institution to record the facts before demanding a fight?
### Reader Questions
Will the Alliance receive the petition, and who is allowed to decide whether Gojin's challenge can proceed?
### Stakes
Petition custody, Qingfeng's public credibility, Taeui's bounded authority, and whether Gojin can turn an evidence question into a private spectacle.
## Chapter Arc
Gojin repeats his charge of theft and overreach before the outer-court crowd. Taeui separates the circulated petition from any final finding, identifies what the documents do and do not prove, and sends the petition through the Alliance intake handoff with receipt and custody marked. When Gojin presses the duel as a public answer, Taeui refuses to treat the request as an automatic judgment: the challenge is recorded for competent review, while acceptance, conditions, and combat remain unresolved. The chapter closes on the challenge file held between Gojin's token and the petition seal.
