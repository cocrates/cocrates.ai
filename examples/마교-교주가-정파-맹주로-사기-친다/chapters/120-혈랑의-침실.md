## Reader Engagement
- **Personal Stake:** A single overstatement can turn a procedural record into an unjust arrest.
- **Opening Question:** What can a witness and four originals honestly establish when each source has a different limit?
- **Reader Questions:** Which entries match, which remain only claimed, and who is allowed to call the next step a judgment?
- **Stakes:** Public trust, civilian relief, record integrity, and the boundary between inquiry and punishment.
