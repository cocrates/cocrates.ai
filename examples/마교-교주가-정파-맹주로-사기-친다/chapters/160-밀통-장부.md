## Purpose
Close the procedural intake beat without turning a draft into receipt, access, review, result, responsibility, guilt, penalty, or disposition.

## Reader Engagement
### Personal Stake
Taeui must make the record exact enough to survive pressure without making it say more than anyone is authorized to know.
### Opening Question
Can an unreceived request be recorded without quietly becoming a fact?
### Stakes
One substituted status word could create false access, false custody, or false blame.

## Chapter Arc
Taeui identifies the numbered Chapter 160 draft and separates its label from every substantive field. Jin seals the procedural state while refusing authorization. Cheongun confirms that no approved physical/custody scope exists and therefore gives no physical report. The record closes with the separate Chapter 161 draft still unapproved and unreceived.
