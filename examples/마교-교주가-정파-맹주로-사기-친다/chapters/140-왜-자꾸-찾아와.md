## Chapter Arc
The chapter begins with the two requests arriving together but not merging. Taeui creates two intake rows and classifies each packet by its stated scope. Cheongun adds only observable condition, while Jin removes language that would turn receipt or physical marks into a conclusion. The chapter closes with two sealed files and a 141 request for a separately approved comparison schedule.
