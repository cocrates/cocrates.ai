## Purpose
To register and classify the separate Chapter 166 placeholder in a second procedural sitting, while explicitly refusing to treat a carried blank line as carried content, access, evidence, responsibility, guilt, penalty, or disposition.

## Opening Question
When a blank line is carried from one sitting to the next, what does the record carry forward?

## Chapter Arc
The chapter opens with the 166 line persisting beneath the sealed 165 procedural row. Taeui has the record staff confirm that a carried line carries no content, then classifies the row as a separate placeholder; Jin seals only the current procedural state without authorizing access or execution; Cheongun confirms that no approved physical/custody scope exists. The blank is closed as a procedural state, not as a substantive answer. The next number, 167, is entered separately and left blank and unsealed.
