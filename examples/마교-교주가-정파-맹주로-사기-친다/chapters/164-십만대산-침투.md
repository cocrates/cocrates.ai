## Reader Engagement
### Personal Stake
The officials must preserve a blank record under pressure to make it look complete; one false word would assign a fact no one is authorized to know.
### Opening Question
Can a number be received without receiving what the number is supposed to contain?
### Reader Questions
What exactly has been received, what remains unreceived, and who has authority to change the blank fields?
### Stakes
Procedural precision is the only safeguard against inventing a route, actor, custody fact, or judgment.

## Chapter Arc
The chapter opens with Taeui separating the number from the request itself. He then records receipt and classification while leaving every substantive field blank, and Jin seals only that procedural state. Cheongun confirms the absence of an approved physical/custody scope without making a report. The chapter closes by separating the three status phrases and placing a numbered but unreceived 165 placeholder outside the 164 record.
