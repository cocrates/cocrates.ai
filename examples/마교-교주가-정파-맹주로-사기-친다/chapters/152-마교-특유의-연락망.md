## Reader Engagement (Chapter Level)
### Personal Stake
Taeui must keep a process precise when everyone around a blank field wants to read a conclusion into it.
### Opening Question
When a request arrives already shaped like a conclusion, who has the authority to stop it at the level of a request?
### Reader Questions
- Which fields can be recorded without granting access?
- Who may seal the procedural state, and who may not decide the substance?
- Can the next request remain separate from the present one?
### Stakes
A single merged line could turn an unapproved request into an unauthorized act or an unjust accusation.

## Chapter Arc
Taeui receives the separate request as a procedural entry and splits its fields. The record desk preserves blanks rather than filling them with implications. Jin seals only the unapproved state and keeps substantive judgment. Cheongun confirms that no approved physical/custody scope exists, so no report is made. The chapter closes with a separately numbered but unapproved Chapter 153 request, not as a result but as the next procedural hold.

##### Scene 1 — 요청이라는 한 칸
**POV:** 남궁태의 | **Location:** 감사 기록실 | **When:** Chapter 151 immediately after
**On stage:** 남궁태의, 기록 담당자

##### Scene 2 — 보고할 수 없는 것
**POV:** 남궁태의 | **Location:** 맹주실 앞 회랑 | **When:** same evening
**On stage:** 남궁태의, 남궁진, 청운
**Staging:** none

- **Situation:** Taeui explains the separated request and asks for the next authority decision; no approved physical/custody scope exists.
- **Beat:** Jin states that he will authorize neither access nor execution and will seal only the procedural state. Cheongun states that, without an approved scope, there is no physical/custody report to make. Taeui keeps result, responsibility, guilt, penalty, and disposition outside the row.
- **Turn:** Authority is clarified by refusal: Jin retains substantive judgment, Cheongun stays within observation limits, and Taeui remains classifier and drafter.
- **Function:** Prevent the request's existence from becoming permission, and prevent non-reporting from becoming evidence.
- **Sensory-emotional:** The corridor lamp leaves the record's lower boxes in shadow → Taeui treats the shadow as an unfilled field, not a hidden fact.
- **Dialogue intent:** Jin narrows the seal in a calm command; Cheongun gives a concrete boundary report; Taeui confirms the limits with restrained irony.
- **Transition out:** They return the row to the record desk for a final status entry, leaving every substantive result blank.
- **Est. length:** 1,850 Korean characters
