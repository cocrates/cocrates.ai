## Purpose

## Reader Engagement (Chapter Level)
### Personal Stake
Taeui must keep the record exact when a blank procedural state invites others to treat silence as a finding.
### Opening Question
Can a numbered request be received without its number becoming an event?
### Reader Questions
What exactly has entered the record, and what remains outside it? Who has authority to move the blank fields forward?
### Stakes
A careless verb could convert non-receipt, non-approval, or non-execution into an invented cause, route, actor, or judgment.

## Chapter Arc
The chapter opens with the 163 placeholder still outside the intake register. Taeui receives and classifies the request at the procedural level, then separates every downstream field. Jin seals only that state. Cheongun confirms that no approved physical/custody scope exists and therefore supplies no physical report. The chapter closes by numbering a separate 164 intake placeholder without receiving or executing it.
