## Purpose
To register the separate Chapter 167 placeholder as a row with its own current procedural state on the third day of the record, while explicitly refusing to treat planting, elapsed dates, registration, or sealing as receipt, content, access, evidence, responsibility, guilt, penalty, or disposition.

## Opening Question
When a placeholder is left open and then registered on a later day, what does the register actually gain?

## Chapter Arc
The chapter opens with the 167 line persisting beneath the sealed 166 procedural row on the third day. Taeui has the record staff confirm that the carried line carries no content and that accumulated dates are not accumulated content, then registers the row as a separate procedural state; Jin seals only the now-existing current state without authorizing access or execution; Cheongun confirms that no approved physical/custody scope exists. The blank is closed as a procedural state, not as a substantive answer. The next number, 168, is entered separately and left blank and unsealed.

**POV:** 남궁태의 | **Location:** procedural record space | **When:** the day after the 166 procedural row was sealed
**On stage:** 남궁태의, 기록 담당자
