## Purpose
The unapproved row is processed through the existing authorization chain: receipt of request, scope correction, limited access, observable custody/condition report, and sealed procedural record. The chapter demonstrates that a completed bounded procedure can still leave the substantive blanks untouched.

## Reader Engagement
- **Opening question:** What may be done when a request has arrived but permission has not?
- **Personal stake:** Taeui must prevent an administrative blank from becoming an accusation; Jin must authorize enough to make the record useful without authorizing a conclusion; Cheongun must describe what is present without explaining why.
- **Stakes:** A procedural record may be relied upon by others, so an overbroad word can become an unjust responsibility claim.
- **Closing hook:** A newly numbered Chapter 149 review row is left separately unapproved, with its substantive fields blank.

## Chapter Arc
An unapproved row is received as a request, not treated as permission. Taeui separates its fields, Jin authorizes only bounded access, and Cheongun performs the permitted physical/custody report. The record is sealed with distinct blank/result statuses. A new unapproved row for Chapter 149 is planted without using it.
