## Threads This Chapter

## Purpose
Show that a procedural frame can be made more exact without turning a pending draft into an event or a blank into a finding.

## Reader Engagement
### Personal Stake
If a blank is treated as proof, Taeui, Jin, and Cheongun's authority boundaries collapse and T-1/Namgung Hwi's hold can be contaminated by inference.

### Opening Question
Can Taeui arrange the record without allowing the record to invent what has not happened?

### Stakes
Procedural integrity, authority separation, and the safety of unresolved holds.

## Chapter Arc
Taeui lays out the separate status fields for the Chapter 161 draft. He refuses to fill its substantive blanks, Jin seals only that refusal and the current state, and Cheongun confirms that no approved physical/custody scope exists without making a report. The chapter closes with a separately numbered Chapter 162 draft left at the same boundary.
