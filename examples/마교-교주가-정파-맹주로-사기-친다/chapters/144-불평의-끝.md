# Chapter 144: 빈 칸의 순서

## Audit Scope
- **Authority:** `audit-serial/143-summary.md`, `audit-serial/cumulative-thread-ledger.md`, `audit-serial/cumulative-character-ledger.md` only.
- **Exclusions:** Later canonical continuity, `continuity/`, and `TODO.md` are not used.
- **Mode:** Sequential procedural access/custody review.

## Prior Audit Alignment
- The separate 143 disposition-scope request draft is carried forward as a request, not as approval or execution.
- Request, access authorization, execution, custody, physical condition, comparison result, responsibility, and final disposition remain separate fields.
- `비교 결과 없음`, `확인 자료 없음`, and `판정 보류` remain distinct.
- Taeui classifies and drafts; Jin narrows scope, authorizes only the bounded access, seals the record, and retains substantive judgment; Cheongun reports observable condition and custody only.
- T-1 and Namgung Hwi remain absent, unresolved, and outside the record.

## Purpose
Process the 143 disposition-scope request through a bounded access and custody review without converting permission, custody, or a physical mark into responsibility or guilt.

## Chapter Arc
An unapproved request is received and split into independent fields. Jin authorizes access to designated procedural fields only. Taeui records what the access did and did not produce while Cheongun reports the packet's observable condition and custody. The final disposition remains blank, and a separate 145 custody-scope request is planted without approval.

- No later identity, route, supplier, faction, clue, or resolution is introduced for T-1 or Namgung Hwi.

### Episode 002: 허가된 칸
#### Key Events
##### Scene 2 — 접근과 보관
**POV:** 남궁태의 | **Location:** 감사 기록실 | **When:** 같은 검토 시간
**On stage:** 남궁태의, 남궁진, 청운, 기록 담당자
**Staging:** audit-record-table
- **Situation:** A request has been numbered, but no access or execution has yet occurred.
- **Beat:** Jin authorizes access only to designated procedural fields and names the excluded fields. Taeui reads and records the authorized fields. Cheongun examines only seal, folds, wetness, pressure, surface condition, and custody, then returns the packet to the recorded custody location.
- **Turn:** Authorized access and observable custody are both recorded as limited acts; neither produces a comparison result or substantive conclusion.
- **Function:** Advances the review while making authority and evidence boundaries visible through action.
- **Sensory-emotional:** The seal's dry ridge, the packet's damp corner, and the clerk's repeated page turns make the narrowness of the permission physically present.
- **Dialogue intent:** Jin states limits; Taeui tests each verb for overclaiming; Cheongun reports only what he can see and handle under the approved scope.
- **Transition out:** The result table remains empty, forcing a separate status pass.
- **Est. length:** 2,400자

### Episode 003: 남겨 둔 서명
