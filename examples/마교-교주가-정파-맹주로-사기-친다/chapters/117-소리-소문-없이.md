## Design-First Revision
Chapter 116 leaves two equally live intake fields: `원본 제출` and `인계 공백 사유 제출`. This chapter selects neither in advance. The opening event is limited to the material actually brought to the intake table: an unnamed transport function submits an original handoff-book bundle. The bundle contains an original handoff page, a custody/location note, and a partial received/transferred time row. It does not contain a separate route-mark sheet or a complete time sequence. Those absences are recorded as `미제출/확인 보류`; they are not converted into concealment, forgery, responsibility, or guilt. The handoff-gap response field remains unused, not rejected or proved false.

The submitted pages are compared against the three separated source classes from 115: the dispatch original, resident receipt testimony, and water-blurred allocation-sheet-like paper. A matching mark is only a match on the page; it does not establish actual dispatch or receipt. The two observed cart directions remain unselected because the submitted bundle contains no route-mark record.

Authority remains divided. Taeui asks, compares, classifies, and records. Jin approves the bounded intake and comparison procedure, while retaining final judgment and sealing. Cheongun confirms seal condition, intake time, custody, folds, wetness, and pressure marks only; he does not infer authorship, forgery, route, or culpability. T-1 and Namgung Hwi remain absent and unresolved.

## Purpose
Make the first actual submission legible without turning the missing portions into a criminal finding. End with a formal receipt, a list of unsubmitted requested materials, and a Chapter 118 pressure hook: the record office must issue an official trace request or explain the record gap through the same bounded procedure.

### Design
- **POV:** 남궁태의

- **Beats:** Taeui opens the intake procedure; Cheongun records seal, time, custody, and physical condition; Jin limits the procedure to receipt and comparison.

## Episode 002: 빈 칸의 이름

- **Location:** 기록 접수실
