## Purpose
Confirm the document-bounded scale and destination of the stolen elixirs, then make Taeui's recovery declaration without entering or controlling Gu Market.

## Reader Engagement (Chapter Level)
### Personal Stake
Cheongun can name small sects whose relief medicine never arrived; the empty delivery lines are not abstract figures.
### Opening Question
If the nine seals are genuine, who received the medicine that the ledgers say was delivered?
### Reader Questions
Which parts are directly proven, how much was diverted, and what can Taeui legitimately recover?
### Stakes
If the separate records are collapsed into an accusation, the recovery plan loses its footing and the real recipients disappear behind a larger scandal.

## Chapter Arc
Ryang begins with the nine-seal sheet and refuses to call it authorization. Taeui matches the Alliance dispatch ledger to clinic, private-storage, and exchange records, bounding 37 missing boxes without assigning all of them to one person. Cheongun connects the missing quantities to real relief recipients. The group separates confirmed recovery from unresolved responsibility, and Taeui ends by declaring that he will take back what was stolen. Gu Market remains outside the chapter; 091 owns the declaration's immediate consequences.
