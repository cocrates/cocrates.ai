## Purpose
To audit the separate Chapter 149 review row without allowing a request or custody report to become approval, substantive review, result, or responsibility.

## Reader Engagement
- **Personal Stake:** Taeui must leave a useful record without borrowing Jin's judgment or Cheongun's eyes.
- **Opening Question:** When a blank row demands an answer, who has the authority to put a mark in it?
- **Reader Questions:** What exactly is being requested, what may be seen, and what will remain blank?
- **Stakes:** An overbroad mark could turn procedure into accusation; an honest blank must survive pressure.

## Chapter Arc
The blank row arrives as an urgent request. Taeui separates its fields and asks for a decision without making one. Jin authorizes only designated physical/custody access, and Cheongun supplies a narrow observable report. The record closes with distinct status phrases and a new Chapter 150 row that remains unapproved.

- **Opening Question:** Who can authorize the next mark?

#### Literary Craft
- **Motifs:** Blank boxes, dry brush, unopened seal.
- **Dialogue Voices:** Taeui uses short, cutting questions; the record keeper reads exact field names; Jin later answers in narrow directives.
- **Reader-Discovered Meaning:** The first act of control is naming what has not happened.
- **Hold:** Any conclusion about the underlying matter.
#### Exposition Budget & Seeds
- **Budget:** Explain only the row's fields as they are handled.
- **Seeds:** Plant=the six blank substantive fields; Hint=pressure to convert request into review.

### Episode 002: 보이는 것의 한계
#### Purpose
Show the authorized physical/custody execution without promoting observation into cause or result.
#### Reader Engagement
- **Personal Stake:** Cheongun must report enough to preserve custody while refusing the meaning others want him to provide.
- **Opening Question:** What can a hand actually know from a sealed object?
- **Reader Questions:** Will physical marks be turned into a story?
- **Stakes:** The report can protect the record only if it stays smaller than inference.
#### Literary Craft
- **Motifs:** Fingertips, seal, custody tag.
- **Dialogue Voices:** Cheongun speaks in measured observable nouns; Taeui paraphrases only to test scope; Jin interrupts overclaiming.
- **Reader-Discovered Meaning:** Precision is visible in what the report declines to name.
- **Hold:** Cause, timing, movement, route, transporter, authorship, and responsibility.
#### Exposition Budget & Seeds
- **Budget:** List only seal, folds, wetness, pressure, surface condition, and current custody.
- **Seeds:** Plant=bounded execution is not substantive review; Hint=the result column remains untouched.
- **Hold:** Comparison and authentication.
#### Key Events
##### Scene 1 — 봉인 보관실
**POV:** 청운 | **Location:** 봉인 보관실 | **When:** 같은 날, authorization 직후
**On stage:** 청운, 남궁태의, 기록 담당자
**Staging:** custody-inspection-table
- **Situation:** The limited authorization permits designated physical/custody fields only; the object remains sealed within current custody.
- **Beat:** Cheongun checks and dictates the seal, folds, wetness, pressure, surface condition, and current custody. Taeui asks whether any item supports a cause or movement statement; Cheongun declines beyond observation.
- **Turn:** The executed action is recorded as a bounded physical/custody check, not as review execution or comparison.
- **Function:** Make the custody boundary concrete and defensible.
- **Sensory-emotional:** Cool paper edge and faint dampness touch Cheongun's fingers → he slows his report so sensation does not become explanation.
- **Dialogue intent:** Cheongun states observable facts; Taeui probes the boundary; the record keeper captures exact wording without interpretation.
- **Transition out:** The physical report returns to the record room with all substantive fields still blank.
- **Est. length:** 1,250 Korean characters

### Episode 003: 남겨 둔 칸
#### Purpose
Close the audit by preserving status distinctions and planting a separate unapproved Chapter 150 row.
#### Reader Engagement
- **Personal Stake:** Taeui accepts an incomplete record as the only honest record available.
- **Opening Question:** Can a blank be made legible without being filled?
- **Reader Questions:** What will the next row demand, and who will own its decision?
- **Stakes:** The next procedural pressure must not inherit authority it has not received.
#### Literary Craft
- **Motifs:** Three status stamps, six blank fields, the seal's final sound.
- **Dialogue Voices:** Taeui names distinctions; Jin confirms custody and judgment hold; the record keeper reads the next row without embellishment.
- **Reader-Discovered Meaning:** Responsibility begins with refusing to assign it to the wrong layer.
- **Hold:** Any final or substantive conclusion.
#### Exposition Budget & Seeds
- **Budget:** Only the three status distinctions and next-row boundary.
- **Seeds:** Plant=separate Chapter 150 row, unapproved and unexecuted.
- **Hint:** Future pressure may ask for the blank to become a result.
- **Hold:** All T-1/Namgung Hwi content and substantive adjudication.
#### Key Events
##### Scene 1 — 세 가지 없음
**POV:** 남궁태의 | **Location:** 감사 기록실 | **When:** same evening
**On stage:** 남궁태의, 남궁진, 기록 담당자
**Staging:** audit-desk-row
- **Situation:** The bounded report is sealed, but the row needs a status record that does not collapse missing evidence into a negative finding.
- **Beat:** Taeui dictates `비교 결과 없음`, `확인 자료 없음`, and `판정 보류` as separate entries; Jin confirms that none is a responsibility or guilt finding; the record keeper leaves result, responsibility, and final disposition blank.
- **Turn:** The absence of a comparison result, absence of confirming material, and pending judgment become visibly different states.
- **Function:** Deliver the chapter's procedural resolution without adjudication.
- **Sensory-emotional:** Three stamps land at different intervals → Taeui hears the gaps between them as carefully as the marks.
- **Dialogue intent:** Taeui insists on semantic separation; Jin confirms authority limits; the record keeper repeats exact labels.
- **Transition out:** The sealed record is placed in custody, and the next row is brought forward only as a request.
- **Est. length:** 900 Korean characters
