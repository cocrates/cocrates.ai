## Purpose
To compare the two preserved records against market and village facts, then prepare a source-separated petition without exceeding what the evidence proves.

## Reader Engagement (Chapter Level)
### Personal Stake
If the packet is misread or lost, Qingfeng's remaining defense may be buried beneath the same accusation that already trapped it.
### Opening Question
What does the blank counterpart field conceal, and can Taeui use it without pretending to know its answer?
### Reader Questions
Which goods actually crossed the Jeomchang market, whose receipt space stayed empty, and why was the accusation written after the movement?
### Stakes
Evidence credibility, Cheongun's fragile return to Qingfeng, and whether a petition can reach a reader before its exhibits are suppressed.

## Chapter Arc
Immediately after the warehouse withdrawal, Taeui separates the original ledger from the packet again and marks what each proves. At the Jeomchang market he matches dates and quantities to empty sacks and shop-side movement without naming the unseen trader. At Qingfeng's village he records residents' losses and checks that the accusation followed the material disappearance. He then writes a petition in layers: verified facts, witness statements, and unanswered questions. Three sealed copies are prepared, but no broad dissemination or verdict occurs; the 073 hook is the first handoff to its routes.
