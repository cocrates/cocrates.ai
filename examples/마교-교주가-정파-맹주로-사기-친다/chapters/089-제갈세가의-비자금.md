## Purpose
Connect the separately wrapped needle to a bounded, source-separated record of Jegal Un's family money without entering or controlling Gu Market, then hand nine-school evidence to 090.

## Reader Engagement (Chapter Level)
### Personal Stake
The missing medicine belongs to small schools like Cheongun's, while the public winner's new evidence can disappear if it is folded into the wrong ledger.
### Opening Question
If the needle did not name its source, what record can show where Jegal Un's money went?
### Reader Questions
Which figures are genuinely missing, what does the Gu Market mark mean, and why are nine seals on one transfer sheet?
### Stakes
A false source claim would destroy the investigation; a lost financial record would leave the injured and small schools without a recoverable trail.

## Chapter Arc
Taeui keeps the recovered needle separate and uses it only as an indexing clue. Ryang compares tournament, relief, and Jegal family records, finding a repeated amount and a missing destination without declaring guilt. At a family warehouse they recover a ledger and transfer sheet: the warehouse moved goods toward an unentered Gu Market account, but the market is only a next site, not a proven source or controlled territory. The final sheet carries nine school seals, opening 090's confirmation of the wider theft.
