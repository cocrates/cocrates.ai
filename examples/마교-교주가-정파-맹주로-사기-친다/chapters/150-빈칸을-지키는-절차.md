### Bound State
- The Chapter 150 row is a separate, numbered, unapproved review request.
- Approval, access, execution, result, responsibility, and final disposition remain blank.
- The prior Chapter 149 bounded physical/custody report is not reopened or broadened.
- `비교 결과 없음`, `확인 자료 없음`, and `판정 보류` remain distinct; none becomes guilt or responsibility.
- Taeui classifies and drafts; Jin decides the authorization boundary and retains substantive judgment; Cheongun reports only observable physical/custody facts when an approved scope exists; record staff preserve fields without inference.

## Purpose
To close the procedural review-row beat by recording that the Chapter 150 row remains unapproved and substantively blank, while planting a separate Chapter 151 hook without converting the row into guilt or responsibility.

## Reader Engagement
### Personal Stake
Taeui must keep an administrative blank from being mistaken for an answer; Jin must prevent procedure from borrowing the authority of judgment.
### Opening Question
Can a numbered row remain only a request when everyone wants its blanks filled?
### Reader Questions
Who will authorize the row, if anyone? What can be recorded without turning absence of a result into a result?
### Stakes
The integrity of the record, the authority boundary among Taeui, Jin, and Cheongun, and the held status of T-1 and Namgung Hwi.

## Chapter Arc
Taeui receives and decomposes the separate 150 row. Jin narrows the record back to its actual status and refuses to let an unapproved row become access or execution. The record staff close the row with blank substantive fields, while a new 151 request is planted as unapproved and separate.
