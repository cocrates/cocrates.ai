## Reader Engagement (Chapter Level)
### Personal Stake

### Reader Questions

## Chapter Arc
The request arrives as a shortcut. Taeui separates its four requested fields. Cheongun confirms only the physical condition of the existing sealed records and explicitly records non-execution. Jin removes wording that could imply permission, leaves the request unsigned and unapproved, and opens a future 143 procedural request without authorizing any act.
