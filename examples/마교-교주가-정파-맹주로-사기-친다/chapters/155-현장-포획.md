## Purpose
To formally receive and classify the Chapter 155 request, preserve Jin's authorization boundary, and leave every substantive and custody field unpopulated.

## Reader Engagement
### Personal Stake
The integrity of the record is at risk whenever a blank is mistaken for an answer.
### Opening Question
When a request arrives with its most important fields empty, who has the authority to decide what the blank means?
### Reader Questions
- Will Taeui turn receipt into permission?
- Will Jin seal a conclusion or only the procedure?
- What will the next numbered request ask for?
### Stakes
A single overbroad word could become false access, custody, responsibility, or guilt in the record.

## Chapter Arc
Taeui receives the separate 155 request and refuses to let its request language imply access or findings. He classifies each procedural layer, brings the authorization question to Jin, and records Jin's refusal to authorize access or execution while sealing only the unapproved state. Cheongun confirms that no approved physical/custody scope exists and therefore makes no report. The record staff preserve the three distinct status phrases and blank substantive fields. The chapter closes on a separately numbered, unapproved 156 request draft.
