## Chapter Arc
The medical curtain gives way to the record desk. Taeui makes the clerks read the direct-application clause, presents his existing supervisory record only as filing accountability, and accepts the preliminary-round condition. The referee registers him without awarding a win or rewriting the suspended match. Cheongun remains under observation. The official call summons Taeui's first opponent to the line, where 084 can begin the rapid bout.
