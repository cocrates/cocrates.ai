## Purpose
To formally intake and classify the separate Chapter 157 request without turning its title or blank fields into a finding about any elder.

## Reader Engagement
### Personal Stake
One unchecked procedural word could become a false accusation, while one omitted field could later be mistaken for a conclusion.
### Opening Question
Can a request titled as an accusation remain only a request when everyone wants its blank fields filled?
### Reader Questions
- What exactly has been received, and what has not been authorized?
- Who may classify, who may seal, and who may report?
- Why is the next request numbered before this one has a result?
### Stakes
The record must preserve uncertainty without allowing silence, non-access, or non-reporting to become guilt or responsibility.

## Chapter Arc
Taeui receives the separate request and splits its title from its procedural fields. Jin refuses to convert a seal into permission or judgment, while Cheongun confirms that no approved physical/custody scope exists and therefore makes no report. The record closes with a separate, unapproved Chapter 158 hook.

#### Key Events
##### Scene 1 — 제목과 요청
**POV:** 남궁태의 | **Location:** audit-office / intake-desk | **When:** 156 다음 감사일
**On stage:** 남궁태의, 기록 담당자
**Staging:** none

- **Situation:** The separately numbered Chapter 157 request from the prior audit is presented with a charged title and blank substantive fields.
- **Beat:** Taeui records receipt, requester-provided title, requested purpose, and classification separately; he marks authorization, access, execution, custody, review, result, responsibility, guilt, penalty, and final disposition as unapproved or blank rather than filling them by inference.
- **Turn:** The title is preserved as a request label only, so the record contains an intake event but no allegation finding.
- **Function:** Establish the procedural boundary and opening tension.
- **Sensory-emotional:** Dry brush hairs catch on the blank approval line → Taeui slows his hand instead of completing it.
- **Dialogue intent:** Taeui asks neutral field-confirmation questions; the record staff confirms entries without interpreting them.
- **Transition out:** The classified sheet is carried to Jin for a procedural decision.
- **Est. length:** 1,650 Korean characters

##### Scene 2 — 봉인의 범위
**POV:** 남궁태의 | **Location:** audit-office / sealed-record-room | **When:** same day, later
**On stage:** 남궁태의, 남궁진, 청운
**Staging:** none

- **Situation:** Jin receives the classified sheet while the authorization and execution fields remain empty.
- **Beat:** Taeui reports the field separation; Jin states that he authorizes no access or execution, seals only the existing procedural state, and retains substantive judgment; Cheongun confirms that no approved physical/custody scope exists and therefore makes no physical/custody report.
- **Turn:** The seal closes the record's current procedural state without becoming permission, access, custody, review, result, responsibility, guilt, penalty, or disposition.
- **Function:** Preserve Taeui, Jin, and Cheongun's distinct authority.
- **Sensory-emotional:** Wax cools along the seal edge → Taeui watches the unfilled result line rather than the emblem.
- **Dialogue intent:** Jin uses narrow authoritative wording; Taeui tests each possible overclaim; Cheongun states only scope absence.
- **Transition out:** Taeui returns the sealed sheet for final status notation.
- **Est. length:** 1,750 Korean characters

##### Scene 3 — 다음 번호
**POV:** 남궁태의 | **Location:** audit-office / intake-desk | **When:** same night
**On stage:** 남궁태의, 청운, 기록 담당자
**Staging:** none

- **Situation:** The sealed Chapter 157 record returns with no substantive result and the three status phrases kept distinct.
- **Beat:** Taeui directs the staff to record `비교 결과 없음`, `확인 자료 없음`, and `판정 보류` in their separate fields; he confirms that non-approval, non-access, non-execution, and non-reporting are not responsibility or guilt. A separate Chapter 158 request draft is numbered with approval, access, execution, custody, review, result, responsibility, guilt, penalty, and final-disposition fields blank.
- **Turn:** The next hook exists only as an unapproved request, while T-1 and Namgung Hwi remain absent, held, and unresolved.
- **Function:** Close with forward pressure without resolving the present request.
- **Sensory-emotional:** Two forms cast overlapping shadows under one lamp → Taeui leaves both result lines untouched.
- **Dialogue intent:** Taeui gives recording instructions; Cheongun confirms no new physical report; the record staff reads back only the written statuses.
- **Transition out:** End on the two numbered forms, with no substantive field completed.
- **Est. length:** 1,450 Korean characters
