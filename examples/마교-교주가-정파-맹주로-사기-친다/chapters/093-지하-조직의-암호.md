## Reader Engagement (Chapter Level)

### Opening Question

### Reader Questions

### Stakes

## Chapter Arc
The two lantern turns force Taeui to compare the visible hand pattern with the old signal structure he remembers, but he refuses to treat resemblance as proof of descent or command. He repeats only a deliberately incomplete movement, watches the gatekeepers adjust distance and route, and tests the market's response with ordinary trader behavior. The market tolerates him as a conditional route-holder, not an owner. At the west stair he sees the gambling hall's entrance rule and hears the synchronized dice above; he stops before the operation begins, leaving 094 the first move inside.
