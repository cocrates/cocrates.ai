## Reader Engagement (Chapter Level)
### Personal Stake
The injured participants need the public record to recognize what happened, while Taeui must win without letting the crowd turn evidence into an uncontrolled sentence.
### Opening Question
Will the referee record what the crowd has seen, or will Jegal Un's public image survive the needle in his hand?
### Reader Questions
What can the recovered needles prove, and why does Taeui keep one instead of handing every object to the officials?
### Stakes
The legitimacy of the tournament result, the injured participants' public standing, and the next investigation.

## Chapter Arc
The referee brings Jegal Un back into the official record and separates the bundle, injury comparisons, and needle recovered during the flight. Jegal Un's denial collapses under the visible sequence of concealment, possession, and escape, but no one claims that the evidence identifies its maker or proves every prior injury. The referee voids Jegal Un's final result and declares Taeui the winner. Taeui refuses the ceremony's weight, keeps one separately wrapped needle as an evidence lead under bounded custody, and chooses Gu Market as the next place to look. Cheongun remains seated and under observation throughout.
