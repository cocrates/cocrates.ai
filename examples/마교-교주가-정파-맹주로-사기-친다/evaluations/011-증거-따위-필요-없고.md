### Required Persona Critique
#### Target Reader
- **Stance:** A mobile wuxia/satisfaction serial reader wants immediate evidence payoff and a concrete next-chapter pull.
- **Strengths:** The 010 promise is paid in the opening lines; the evidence is legible; the raised hand supplies a clean continuation.
- **Defects:** The chapter delays the full catharsis by design, so a reader expecting the leg break immediately may feel the stop.
- **Reader impact:** Low risk because the unfinished famous line and active hand make the delay feel like a cliffhanger, not a stall.

#### Genre Critic
- **Stance:** Tests the chapter against revenge wuxia and family-hearing conventions.
- **Strengths:** Evidence-as-weapon and polite procedural inversion fit the genre promise without skipping causality.
- **Defects:** The bounded evidence makes the chapter less explosively conclusive than the prior draft.
- **Reader impact:** The restraint strengthens the later payoff and avoids an unearned early victory.

#### Reader-Editor
- **Stance:** Tests serialization clarity, density, and end-hook mechanics.
- **Strengths:** Three evidence classes are easy to track; the exact line split is unmistakable; the final image is visual.
- **Defects:** Repeated “확인” exchanges could be tightened in a later prose-polish pass, but no structural change is required.
- **Reader impact:** The repeated procedural word reinforces the hearing’s pressure and remains readable on mobile.

#### Literary Critic
- **Stance:** Tests sentence rhythm, motif control, and emotional indeterminacy.
- **Strengths:** Paper, hand, smile, cane, and shadow carry the chapter without explanatory theme statements. Hwi’s fear remains inferential.
- **Defects:** The chapter’s deliberate restraint leaves no independent emotional release before the hook.
- **Reader impact:** The withheld release is compensated by the exact unfinished sentence and immediate bodily movement.

#### Character Critic
- **Stance:** Tests whether the named roles remain distinct and motivated.
- **Strengths:** Gyeong protects process, Jin withholds judgment, Hyeok manages surface calm, and Hwi’s slip comes from self-preservation rather than sudden confession.
- **Defects:** Jin’s silence gives him limited page movement, but that is the locked role for this hearing span.
- **Reader impact:** The role separation prevents the hearing from becoming a single-voice confrontation.
