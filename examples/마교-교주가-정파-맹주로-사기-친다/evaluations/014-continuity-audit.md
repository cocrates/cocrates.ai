## Critic Personas

#### Target Reader

- **Stance:** Wants fast household-level consequence after the gahoe while needing a concrete next-chapter pull.
- **Strengths:** Hwi's broken leg immediately produces a changed daily routine; Taeui's control is visible without falsely declaring victory.
- **Defects:** The lack of a final punishment may feel restrained. **Severity: Low.** The restraint is the inherited 013 contract and is offset by concrete treatment and custody changes.
- **Reader impact:** The reader gets operational payoff and a live question about who can make the arrangement permanent.

#### Genre Critic

- **Stance:** Tests the wuxia family-power promise and whether the aftermath respects procedure.
- **Strengths:** The injured rival remains dangerous in speech, the physician remains compromised and useful, and authority is shown through narrow commands rather than a lawless total victory.
- **Defects:** Household administration occupies more space than combat. **Severity: Low.** This is the required aftermath chapter and keeps pressure through the uncertain authority above Taeui.
- **Reader impact:** The revenge payoff becomes a durable power arrangement instead of a premature endpoint.

#### Plot Expert

- **Stance:** Checks handoff causality, state certainty, and unresolved-thread discipline.
- **Strengths:** 013's open judgment gap is honored; Hwi's relocation is newly established rather than smuggled in as prior fact; Jin's response remains non-diagnostic.
- **Defects:** Temporary placement and final disposition could be confused by a hurried read. **Severity: Low.** The prose repeats the distinction through the unlocked door and the unassigned decision.
- **Reader impact:** No later procedure is stolen, and 015 has a clear obligation to test or report the arrangement.

#### Reader-Editor

- **Stance:** Checks serial pacing, repetition, and the sellable unit ending.
- **Strengths:** Opens on the physical consequence, turns rules into small household actions, and ends with a concrete empty space rather than a thematic verdict.
- **Defects:** The medicine-record details recur. **Severity: Low.** They are the material mechanism of the chapter's narrow rule and are varied by location and viewpoint reaction.
- **Reader impact:** The chapter feels like consequence rather than recap and leaves a reason to continue.

#### Character Critic

- **Stance:** Checks whether each person acts from the inherited state.
- **Strengths:** Hwi resists being reduced to a defeated prop; No 의원 survives by accountability; Kim Chung remains a constrained asset; Seolhyang receives safety without instant empowerment; Jin and Hyeok preserve opacity.
- **Defects:** Hwi's interior fear remains mostly indirect. **Severity: Low.** The chapter keeps the fixed Taeui POV and lets the injury, threats, and door do the work.
- **Reader impact:** The cast retains pressure and future utility instead of being cleanly sorted into winners and losers.

#### Literary Critic

- **Stance:** Checks material motifs, rhythm, and emotional overstatement.
- **Strengths:** Bandage, medicine box, threshold, unlocked door, and empty seat carry the transition from violence to provisional order. The prose avoids declaring Jin's meaning.
- **Defects:** Administrative instructions create a deliberate middle restraint. **Severity: Low.** Sensory details and servant behavior keep the sequence embodied.
- **Reader impact:** The chapter's quietness reads as unstable aftermath, not resolution.
