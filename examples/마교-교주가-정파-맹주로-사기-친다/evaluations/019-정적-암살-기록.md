## Required Persona Audit
#### Target Reader
- Stance: Values fast evidence-driven revenge, clarity, and a strong next-chapter pull.
- Strengths: The packet opening answers the prior hook quickly; the three-route choice creates a concrete reason to continue.
- Defects: The evidence taxonomy is dense in the middle; severity Low. Fix applied through short repeated distinctions and physical bundle handling.
- Reader impact: The reader can understand what Taeui knows without losing the revenge promise.

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: Checks the web-novel contract of satisfying exposure and anti-hypocrisy momentum.
- Strengths: The chapter advances toward public exposure while preserving a later social-massacre payoff.
- Defects: Full catharsis is deferred; severity Low and intentional because 020 is the dissemination execution chapter.
- Reader impact: The delay is a loaded choice rather than a stall.

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: Checks mobile pacing, scene handoffs, exposition density, and serial hooks.
- Strengths: Each episode changes the evidence state; the final image leaves an actionable choice.
- Defects: The three bundle explanations could slow scrolling; severity Low. Physical actions and dialogue breaks were retained to keep the information moving.
- Reader impact: The reader receives procedural clarity without a chapter-ending summary lecture.

#### Character Critic
- Stance: Checks motivation, voice, and pressure among Taeui, Hyeok, Jin, Ryang, Gyeong, Seolhyang, and Kim Chung.
- Strengths: Taeui's restraint is strategic rather than passive; Ryang and Gyeong have distinct evidentiary roles; Jin's refusal preserves ambiguity.
- Defects: Hyeok is offstage; severity Low and appropriate to the chapter's document-audit purpose.
- Reader impact: The absent antagonist remains a pressure source through the handwriting evidence rather than being prematurely exposed in dialogue.

#### Literary Critic
- Stance: Checks rhythm, motifs, sensory detail, dialogue music, and reader-discovered meaning.
- Strengths: Seals, closed doors, hands, and separated bundles carry the chapter's meaning without a thematic closing monologue.
- Defects: Repeated boundary statements risk slight didacticism; severity Low. They are distributed through action, not left as a final explanation.
- Reader impact: The reader is invited to judge the gap between a trace and a verdict.
