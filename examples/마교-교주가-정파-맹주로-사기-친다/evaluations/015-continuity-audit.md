## Critic Findings

- **Strengths:** The reader receives a concrete aftermath, a usable information network, Seolhyang's payoff, and a clean final hook.
- **Defect:** The absence of a final Hwi punishment may feel less explosive. **Severity: Low.** It is required by the 014 boundary and is replaced by procedural tension.

#### Genre Critic

- **Strengths:** The family-cleaning payoff remains visible through ledgers, custody, and daily control; the next threat arrives through a familiar courteous gesture.
- **Defect:** T-1 is deliberately not pursued. **Severity: Low.** The unresolved merchant is the specified Part 3/next-stage thread.

#### Plot Expert

- **Strengths:** Every major turn follows from 014, and no unresolved evidence is promoted to fact.
- **Defect:** The connected record network could look more authoritative than it is. **Severity: Low.** The prose repeatedly limits it to today's operation and observed range.

#### Reader-Editor

- **Strengths:** The revised opening begins with action, avoids a recap dump, and the ending is image-led.
- **Defect:** Ledger and medicine checks recur. **Severity: Low.** Repetition is functional: each location shows a different boundary of provisional order.

#### Character Critic

- **Strengths:** Seolhyang gains agency without a rank promotion; No 의원 remains monitored; Kim Chung remains a constrained asset; Hyeok does not disclose himself.
- **Defect:** Hwi is present mainly through records rather than a direct scene. **Severity: Low.** This preserves the requested relocation uncertainty and the fixed Taeui-centered design.

#### Literary Critic

- **Strengths:** Ledger, threshold, medicine steam, empty seat, and applause carry the chapter without a thematic closing statement.
- **Defect:** The quiet administrative middle reduces spectacle. **Severity: Low.** The silence is converted into serial tension by the final applause.
