## Design-First Revision
The design was revised before the manuscript. The chapter now treats Taeui's public statement as an accusation and investigation request, not as an already-proven conclusion. The prescription records are limited to dates, handwriting, alterations, and custody. Kim Chung's statement remains a separate, bounded witness route. The parallel ledger route remains a request for comparison rather than a completed finding. 노의원 is not newly promoted to a proven accomplice; Seolhyang remains safe and uninformed; Taeui's poison heat, short breath, and incomplete repair remain active. The gahoe is opened through Namgung Gyeong's formal elder-mediated authority, and the closing hook requires item-by-item proof in Chapter 011.

## Required Persona Review
#### Target Reader
- **Stance:** Values fast mobile serial escalation, a sharp protagonist, and a concrete next-chapter demand.
- **Strengths:** The accusation lands early, while the three evidence bundles give the reader a clear tactical question rather than a solved mystery.
- **Defect:** The evidence distinctions briefly slow the middle; **Low**. The revision keeps each limit attached to a character action or object.
- **Reader impact:** The reader gets a public confrontation and still has a specific reason to continue into 011.

#### Genre Critic
- **Stance:** Checks the revenge/gahoe contract and escalation within the family-cleaning arc.
- **Strengths:** Taeui turns formalist procedure into pressure, and Hwi's smile visibly fails without premature physical punishment.
- **Defect:** The chapter delays direct punishment; **Low**. This is appropriate because the chapter-list role is the opening of the exposure, not the fall itself.
- **Reader impact:** The serial promises that the next unit will test the evidence rather than reset the confrontation.

#### Reader-Editor
- **Stance:** Checks scroll rhythm, clarity, exposition restraint, and the chapter-unit hook.
- **Strengths:** Three compact movements, recurring paper/seat imagery, and a final itemized demand keep the procedural material readable.
- **Defect:** The sentence explaining the four unproven categories is dense; **Low**. It is retained because the boundary is a continuity-critical obligation and is broken into separate beats.
- **Reader impact:** Confusion risk is controlled: records, witness, and ledger are visibly different objects.

#### Literary Critic
- **Stance:** Checks rhythm, sensory embodiment, motif, and emotional restraint.
- **Strengths:** Paper texture, short breath, fixed seats, and the delayed return of Hwi's smile carry uncertainty without a thematic lecture.
- **Defect:** The formal meeting naturally uses repeated procedural vocabulary; **Low**. Variation comes from physical blocking and Taeui's clipped humor.
- **Reader impact:** The chapter feels like a trap being assembled, not an administrative briefing.
