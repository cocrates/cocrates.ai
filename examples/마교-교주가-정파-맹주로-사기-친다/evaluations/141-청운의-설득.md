### Required Persona Review
#### Target Reader
- **Stance:** Values clear serialized stakes and a next-step question.
- **Strengths:** The blank result fields create tension without confusing a procedural request with a finding; the closing image gives a specific 142 pull.
- **Defects:** Repeated status language may feel deliberate and slow.
- **Reader impact:** Low risk because each repetition changes who is authorized to say or record it.

#### Genre Critic
- **Stance:** Protects the evidence-driven martial-world audit contract.
- **Strengths:** No premature culprit, route, or authenticity reveal; authority conflict supplies the chapter action.
- **Defects:** The chapter withholds a conventional revelation.
- **Reader impact:** Appropriate for the established sequential audit mode; the withheld result is the promised feature, not a missing payoff.

#### Reader-Editor
- **Stance:** Tests serialization clarity, rhythm, and handoff readability.
- **Strengths:** Three episodes have distinct procedural jobs; the 142 hook is legible.
- **Defects:** Some exchanges restate limits.
- **Reader impact:** The restatement reinforces the central evidence boundary and does not introduce a stall.

#### Literary Critic
- **Stance:** Tests sentence rhythm, motif, and emotion without over-labeling.
- **Strengths:** Wet paper, separated seals, and blank fields carry the emotional pressure; the prose avoids a thematic closing lecture.
- **Defects:** The procedural vocabulary narrows tonal range.
- **Reader impact:** The narrow range suits the controlled audit chapter and keeps attention on small authority shifts.
