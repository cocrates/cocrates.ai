## Required Persona Critique

### Target Reader
- Strength: The chapter-to-chapter escalation is highly legible: apology → ledger → assassination record → dissemination → public collapse → family restructure → external target.
- Finding: 030 deliberately delays physical punishment at the external site. **Medium**, but this is the correct hook because the target reader is promised a larger Part 4 payoff rather than a premature resolution. **Apply: no change.**

### Genre Critic
- Strength: The protagonist's demonic-eye investigation and ledger-based social execution honor the wuxia catharsis while varying the Part 2 fist-first payoff.
- Finding: Public shaming could feel procedural if evidence remains abstract. **Medium. Apply:** preserve repeated tactile anchors (red seal, cut page, empty relief bowl) in prose.

### Plot Expert
- Strength: Each chapter has a concrete handoff and no orphaned reveal; 020's dissemination causes 021~025's social cascade.
- Finding: Hyeok's final backlash must not resolve his deeper threat. **High. Apply:** 025 design keeps the backlash as a failed counterstrike and preserves Part 4/15 long-range antagonist potential.

### Reader-Editor
- Strength: Three-episode rhythm and chapter hooks suit mobile serialization; 016 and 030 mirror each other through apology/document and seal/flag.
- Finding: 024's silence risks a low-action dip. **Medium. Apply:** stage silence as a choice with a visible consequence, not exposition.

### Character Critic
- Strength: Hyeok's plausible worldview is social order through controlled scandal; Jin's worldview is balance through non-intervention; Taeui rejects both.
- Finding: Do not label Hyeok a villain in narration before readers see the pattern. **High. Apply:** keep direct villain verdict in Hold; let gesture, omissions, and ledgers carry the conclusion.

### Setting/Lore Expert
- Strength: The new external locations are catalogued and sensory anchors are fixed; the red Murim Alliance seal remains consistent.
- Finding: 030 could accidentally imply all Jeomchang people are corrupt. **Medium. Apply:** retain unnamed officials and individual hungry civilians, while holding the sect-wide verdict.

## Architect Adjudication

All High findings were applied at design level. Medium findings are binding prose cues. The designs are **Architect-approved** for generation.

**Design Verdict: PASS / Manuscript-ready.**
