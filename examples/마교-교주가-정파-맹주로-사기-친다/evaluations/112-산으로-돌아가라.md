- Stance: Serialized martial-arts reader seeking procedural catharsis and a concrete next threat.
- Strengths: The first responsibility question is immediately legible; the public table visibly produces consequences; the final packet gives a specific next-chapter pull.
- Defects: The document mechanics are dense in the middle. Severity Low. Proposed fix: retain tactile actions and keep every explanation attached to a visible column or seal.
- Reader impact: The chapter rewards readers who want Taeui's authority to be demonstrated rather than merely announced.

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: Tests whether institutional confrontation still delivers genre pressure.
- Strengths: Taeui wins ground through precision, not an unsupported power display; orthodox resistance remains a live obstacle.
- Defects: No combat beat appears. Severity Low. Proposed fix: apply none; the audit handoff specifically requires responsibility-table execution, and a fight would dilute the procedural payoff.
- Reader impact: The chapter provides a different form of “압도” consistent with the prior chapter's public-record pressure.

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: Tests serialization clarity, momentum, and next-unit conversion.

- Defects: The opening repeats some table fields. Severity Low. Proposed fix: apply none because repetition demonstrates the question's boundary and prevents a guilt leap.

- Defects: Some exchanges are intentionally blunt. Severity Low. Proposed fix: apply none; clipped speech matches Taeui's established audit voice.
- Reader impact: The procedural scene has physical texture and does not collapse into a transcript.
