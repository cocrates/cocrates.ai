## Design-First Revision
- Restored the handoff as **partial restoration + active poison + concealed condition**.
- Made the first walk physically limited and required Taeui to hide the poison response.
- Converted the household rumor into an observable chain: physician report → servants → Hyeok's public interpretation → Jin's fact-checking visit.
- Kept Hyeok's control tell as a Hint, not confirmation of the Part 3 role.
- Kept Jin's coldness visible while holding his knowledge, motive, and complicity.
- Added Hwi's off-stage reaction hook without showing or confirming his guilt.
- Kept the night-assault staging anonymous and compatible with `stagings/p1-taeui-night-assault.md`.
