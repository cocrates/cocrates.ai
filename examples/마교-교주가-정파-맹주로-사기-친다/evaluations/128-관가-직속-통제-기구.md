### Design Critique
#### Target Reader
- Stance: Values fast serial clarity, procedural suspense, Taeui's short sarcasm, and a concrete next-chapter pull.
- Strengths: The two-envelope visual is immediate; every boundary is dramatized through action rather than a lecture; 129 requests create a clean continuation hook.
- Defects: The deliberately restrained chapter has lower conventional action intensity. Severity Low. Fix: retain the pressure through repeated near-inferences and Taeui's dialogue, without adding a route result.
- Reader impact: Readers understand exactly what is unresolved and why the next review matters.

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: Checks the audit/anti-corruption serial contract and satisfying protagonist control.
- Strengths: Taeui wins a procedural contest by refusing premature accusation; the martial-world flavor remains in the authority conflict and clipped voice.
- Defects: No physical confrontation. Severity Low. Fix: not applied because the requested scope explicitly limits work to access approval and confirmation range.
- Reader impact: The chapter serves as a suspenseful evidence-management turn rather than a payoff chapter.

#### Plot Expert
- Stance: Checks causality, scope, and handoff into 129.
- Strengths: Each episode has a causal turn; approval is not confused with fact; the 129 testimony/physical-trace hook is generated directly from the 128 decision.
- Defects: None material.
- Reader impact: No route is accidentally resolved or merged.

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: Checks mobile readability, density, and end hook.
- Strengths: Short paragraphs, repeated visible fields, and the closing empty handoff line support scrolling; the end state is concrete.
- Defects: Repetition of “확인 자료 없음” is intentional but can feel procedural. Severity Low. Fix: keep because repetition is the chapter's motif and audit requirement.
- Reader impact: The reader can retain the central question without a continuity reset.
