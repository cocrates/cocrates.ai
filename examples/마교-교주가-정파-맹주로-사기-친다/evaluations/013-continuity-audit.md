## Critic Personas

#### Target Reader

- **Stance:** Values fast procedural payoff, Taeui's controlled dominance, and a concrete next-chapter pull.
- **Strengths:** The chapter pays off the ledger hook while retaining the satisfying contrast between Taeui's calm and the elders' anger. The bounded confirmation remains a meaningful reveal rather than a full confession.
- **Defects:** The distinction between record anomaly and actual theft requires attention from the reader. **Severity: Low.** The revised dialogue repeats the boundary once through Gyeong and once through Ryang, enough for serial clarity without resolving the mystery.
- **Reader impact:** The reader receives evidence progress and a live authority conflict, with a concrete reason to continue.

#### Genre Critic

- **Stance:** Tests the wuxia-family-hearing promise and whether the revenge payoff remains earned.
- **Strengths:** Taeui wins control of the question without converting suspicion into omniscient certainty. Gyeong's resistance preserves the genre's honor-and-procedure counterforce.
- **Defects:** The chapter deliberately withholds a culprit after a ledger reveal. **Severity: Low.** This is appropriate because 012's audit boundary still holds the route and attribution open.
- **Reader impact:** The payoff is a sharper battlefield, not an empty delay: evidence now threatens the family's records while authority remains contested.

#### Plot Expert

- **Stance:** Checks causality, escalation, handoffs, and evidence logic.
- **Strengths:** The 012 handoff is direct; the ledger comparison is physically staged; Ryang's confirmation has a precise scope; Gyeong's reservation converts the reveal into a procedural cliff rather than a verdict.
- **Defects:** The chapter has two parallel tensions, Hwi's medical urgency and the ledger proceeding. **Severity: Low.** The prose keeps both visible and lets Gyeong explicitly limit the comparison.
- **Reader impact:** No held thread is accidentally closed, and the 014 obligation is legible.

#### Reader-Editor

- **Stance:** Checks serialization, density, repetition, and end-of-unit pull.
- **Strengths:** The opening starts on pain and authority, not recap. Short exchanges carry evidence limits. The final objects, vacant seat and closed ledger, provide a clean serial image.
- **Defects:** Some procedural distinctions recur. **Severity: Low.** They are necessary safeguards against the original over-certainty and are distributed across action rather than presented as a lecture.
- **Reader impact:** The chapter remains readable as a payoff chapter while making the next decision unavoidable.

#### Character Critic

- **Stance:** Checks whether each character acts from the inherited state rather than serving the audit conclusion.
- **Strengths:** Gyeong protects procedure even while accepting the record check; Ryang protects his office by limiting his claim; Taeui narrows his rule when challenged; Hyeok mediates without revealing a hidden motive; Jin remains unreadable.
- **Defects:** Ryang's emotional reaction is restrained. **Severity: Low.** The restraint fits a public ledger confirmation and avoids turning his role into automatic allegiance.
- **Reader impact:** The authority shifts are more credible because no elder becomes a premature convert.

#### Literary Critic

- **Stance:** Checks rhythm, recurring objects, sensory grounding, and whether the ending explains itself.
- **Strengths:** The staff, ledger, breath, and vacant seat form a coherent material sequence. The prose moves from impact sounds to paper sounds, then closes on absence rather than a verdict.
- **Defects:** The opening physical aftermath and later ledger handling use several short declarative beats. **Severity: Low.** The rhythm is intentional and appropriate to a hearing whose authority keeps stopping and restarting.
- **Reader impact:** The chapter feels tense without requiring an emotional label for every participant.
