# Parts 4–5 Continuity Summary

**Released:** 2026-08-01  
**Scope:** 031~062 / 32 chapters / 148,978 characters

## Part 4 — “마교보다 더한 놈이 나타났다”
- Taeui turns the Jeomchang seal and relief discrepancy into a field investigation.
- He witnesses Jeomchang and Qingcheng extracting money and grain under orthodox legitimacy, then reverses the flow and returns resources to smaller groups.
- Public challenges and lawful loopholes establish Taeui inside the Murim Alliance Youth Corps.
- He uses the Alliance-lord's-son privilege as a weapon against corrupt officials, builds a following, survives great-sect pressure and Namgung Jin's test, and reaches the Youth Corps peak.
- Closing position: Taeui publicly accepts that he is the Alliance-lord's son and dares challengers to defeat him.

## Part 5 — “흙수저 무사와의 첫 만남”
- The Youth Corps sends Taeui to a border patrol.
- At Qingfeng Gate and its village, he meets Cheongun, a poor senior disciple who places himself between bandits and civilians.
- Taeui's profit-based worldview collides with Cheongun's unprofitable protection ethic.
- Taeui recognizes sincerity, mocks the third-rate sword, then rebuilds its mechanics through efficient pressure and stabbing instruction.
- A saboteur assault tests Cheongun; Taeui intervenes with overwhelming demonic efficiency and takes him in despite himself.
