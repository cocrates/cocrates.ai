# Parts 9–17 Continuity Summary

**Released:** 2026-08-01  
**Scope:** 101~200 / 100 chapters / 543,888 characters

## Parts 9–12
- Nine Schools elders' hearing is reversed with black-market ledgers.
- Taeui negotiates the Blood Heaven border crisis in three days through a demonic ultimatum.
- Imperial inspector Lee Jin-sang's corruption is exposed and the 관무쌍청 boundary is restored.
- The Shinmudan forms, trains, rescues civilians, and witnesses an orthodox–Tianmo contact.

## Parts 13–15
- Taeui traces blood-seal contacts, exposes the orthodox/Tianmo pact, and captures the pact ledger.
- He returns to Ten-Thousand Mountains, reclaims Tianmo leadership from the fake Heavenly Demon, and maintains his public identity.
- Namgung Jin and the elders resist; Taeui defeats his father, takes Namgung House, surrounds the Alliance, disables and exiles the corrupt elders.

## Parts 16–17
- The expelled alliance and fake-Tianmo remnants raise a ten-thousand army.
- Shinmudan and true Tianmo soldiers unite; Cheongun commands the orthodox front.
- Taeui fuses Namgung sword and original Tianmo art, wins the war, and founds the Tianxia Martial Alliance.
- The new law judges protection of the weak rather than faction labels. Cheongun becomes practical chief; Taeui ends on a comic new report and departure.

## Final states
- 남궁태의: absolute leader of the Tianxia Martial Alliance and true Tianmo successor; still a restless troublemaker.
- 청운: practical chief and pillar of the new order.
- 남궁진: defeated and politically repurposed under the new order.
- 신무단/천마 진짜 무사: integrated foundation of the new alliance.

## Resolved
