# Parts 6–8 Continuity Summary

**Released:** 2026-08-01

- Qingfeng's medicinal field becomes the target of a false demonic-collusion accusation.
- The Alliance ignores the appeal; Taeui arrives as a Namgung direct investigator.

## Part 7 — 천하무도대회 & 위선자 챔피언 참교육
- The martial tournament introduces Jegal Un, the orthodox youth idol.
- Taeui detects a banned drug and hidden pressure-point destruction harming defeated competitors.
- Cheongun is endangered; Taeui registers and reaches the final through overwhelming efficiency.
- Jegal Un's needle is exposed in public; Taeui wins and leaves one needle as the trail to the Gu Market.

## Part 8 — 강호 흑시장 털어먹기
- The needle leads to Je Gal family slush funds and Nine Schools elder embezzlement.
- Taeui and Cheongun infiltrate the Gu Market, use Taeui's former demonic underworld knowledge, and sweep gambling and auction operations.
- Heukya-cha's assassins fail; Taeui negotiates 70% control rather than destroying the market outright.
- Funds are released under the Alliance name, a gratitude monument is raised, and Nine Schools elders form a hearing alliance.
