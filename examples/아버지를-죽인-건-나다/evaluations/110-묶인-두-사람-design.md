## Design Critique (required personas)

#### Target Reader
- Stance: Adult male regression/martial-arts web-novel reader seeking tactical reversals and father-son payoff.
- Strengths: Opens at the prior cliffhanger; makes the third route measurable rather than sentimental; ends with a visible cost.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The reader can track the choice through line movement and wants to see whether the sacrifice succeeds.

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: Tests regression-wuxia contract, payoff pressure, and action readability.
- Strengths: The protagonist wins initiative by testing a system, while the win carries a real loss.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Delivers tactical satisfaction without erasing danger.

#### Plot Expert
- Stance: Tests causal sequence, hook alignment, and scope discipline.
- Strengths: Scene 1’s observation causes Scene 2’s experiment; Scene 2’s finding causes Scene 3’s rejection; Scene 3’s choice causes Scene 4’s hook.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: No unexplained leap from “bound” to “save both”; the cost is earned through evidence.

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: Tests serial pacing, exposition restraint, and closing density.
- Strengths: The ending has one central obligation, not a crowded stack of revelations; the held material protects 111’s momentum.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Low skim risk and a clean next-click reason.

#### Literary Critic
- Stance: Tests motif and emotional meaning without thematic announcement.
- Strengths: The black line and abandoned sword externalize relationship-as-control and power relinquishment.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The emotional beat remains embodied in action, suitable for the locked fast serial tone.

#### Character Critic
- Stance: Tests whether each person acts from the locked relationship and profile.
- Strengths: Dohyun’s self-sacrifice, the Lord’s ownership language, and Jinwoo’s refusal all follow their established drives and voices.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The triangle advances without prematurely resolving guilt or forgiveness.
