## Design Critique
#### Target Reader
- Stance: Adult male web-novel reader seeking regression advantage, tactical reversals, and family/revenge pressure.
- Strengths: Opens on immediate consequence; converts a name-based accusation into a traceable counter-investigation; the badge image gives a strong click-forward hook.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The reader receives a concrete answer (target and forgery direction) while retaining a sharper companion mystery.

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: Tests regression martial-serial promise and evidence-driven catharsis.
- Strengths: Jinwoo’s advantage is used to read routes, not to solve everything; the false accusation produces a satisfying reversal.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Delivers tactical competence without prematurely cashing the 078 memory reveal.

#### Plot Expert
- Stance: Tests causality, thread separation, Hook alignment, and Out scope.
- Strengths: TH-116 and TH-117 split cleanly; every scene turn causes the next; Out does not add a chase or faction arrival.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Reduces late-arc mystery confusion while preserving the next-episode question.

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: Tests serialization rhythm, exposition restraint, and closing density.
- Strengths: Four scenes each have one evidence job; closing has two linked obligations rather than a pile of new reveals.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Low skim risk and clear next-click motivation.

#### Literary Critic
- Stance: Tests whether motif and closing image are executable rather than author commentary.
- Strengths: Wet ash and empty place are concrete, repeatable images; the closing avoids thematic monologue.
- Defects: Motif touches are episode-level only; preserve both images during generation. (Low; generation constraint.)
- Reader impact: Material imagery gives the procedural investigation emotional residue without slowing it.

#### Character Critic
- Stance: Tests agency, relationship pressure, voice, and profile-backed recognition.
- Strengths: Jinwoo refuses immediate accusation; Hyuk’s procedural brake and Taeseok’s bounded report distinguish the team; 백무진’s established external-haul role supports the evidence trail.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Trust rupture feels personal before the next episode supplies motive.
