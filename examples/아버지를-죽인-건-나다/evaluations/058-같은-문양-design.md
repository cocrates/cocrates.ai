## Design Critique (required personas)
#### Target Reader
- Stance: Adult male regression/murim serial reader seeking revenge, tactical evidence, and father-son reversals.
- Strengths: The public proof converts cleanly into a dangerous destination; the exact hostile appellation is a high-retention Out.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The reader receives a concrete answer advance while the larger truth remains contested.

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: Tests regression murim and family-revenge contract.
- Strengths: Jinwoo wins through evidence and tactical choice rather than omniscient foreknowledge; the father remains morally unresolved.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Preserves the expected combination of investigation, pressure, and delayed revelation.

#### Plot Expert
- Stance: Tests causality, hook alignment, and scope.
- Strengths: 057 evidence causes the inference; the inference causes the journey; the journey causes the witness confrontation. Summary, Out, and Turn align at identical hook strength.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: No causal gap forces the reader to accept a location jump without setup.

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: Tests scene economy, serialization, and closing density.
- Strengths: Each scene has a distinct job; the Out contains one main obligation and one supporting key condition.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The episode can be read as a satisfying evidence step while still selling 059.

#### Literary Critic
- Stance: Tests motif and emotional embodiment.
- Strengths: Bent lines and the silent bell externalize a shared life structure and an unfinished judgment; the design holds back thematic explanation.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The reversal gains emotional weight without stopping the plot for a lecture.

#### Character Critic
- Stance: Tests motive, voice, and profile-backed recognition.
- Strengths: Jinwoo turns disgust into method; Dohyun’s warning and blocking action preserve the silence constraint; Hyuk keeps evidence public; the gatekeeper’s recognition follows his profile.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Each relationship adds pressure to the same decision rather than producing interchangeable dialogue.

#### Setting/Lore Expert
- Stance: Tests blood-seal rules, site credibility, and citeable facets.
- Strengths: The episode uses existing blood-contract rules and exact location anchors; the 폐관’s physical evidence makes the lore legible.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The new ritual information feels discovered rather than invented for exposition.
