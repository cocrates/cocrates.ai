## Design Critique

#### Target Reader
- Stance: The locked adult male web-fiction reader wants the kill-right payoff to feel decisive and wants the father conflict to remain morally sharp.
- Strengths: The forced name reading gives the revenge a concrete spectacle; Jinwoo adding his own and Dohyun’s names prevents a soft forgiveness turn; the poison relapse is a strong next-episode pull.
- Defects: Scene 4’s mother movement conflicts with the fixed 117 staging and could make the visual continuity feel careless → Med → keep her at the waterway while she stops actively bracing it.
- Reader impact: A one-line blocking fix protects immersion at the exact climax without changing the cathartic judgment.
