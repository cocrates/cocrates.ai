## Design Critique (required personas)

#### Target Reader
- **Stance:** Adult male regression-martial-arts reader seeking tactical reversal, sect control, and family betrayal pressure.
- **Strengths:** The episode converts the prior seal discovery into an actionable raid and ends on a potentially devastating Dohyun record.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** Strong click-forward promise: the reader gets both an operational win and a father-related counterattack.

#### Genre Critic
- **Stance:** Tests martial-arts serial promises, earned power, and revenge escalation.
- **Strengths:** Jinwoo wins through seal procedure, personnel placement, and evidence—not an unexplained combat upgrade.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The command-line reversal supplies genre satisfaction while preserving a larger fight for later.

#### Plot Expert
- **Stance:** Tests causality, Hook alignment, and Out scope.
- **Strengths:** The borrowed seal causes the split guard routes; the route leads to the records; the record creates the next question.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The close is a single legible obligation rather than a stack of unrelated reveals.

#### Reader-Editor
- **Stance:** Tests mobile rhythm, procedural drag, and closing density.
- **Strengths:** Each scene changes control: hall → threshold → storehouse; objects replace exposition.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The reader can skim neither the raid nor the record because each physical action changes the evidence state.

#### Literary Critic
- **Stance:** Tests material motif, restraint, and closing image.
- **Strengths:** Hands, seals, empty bowl, and folded paper make authority and omission tactile.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The father conflict remains emotionally active without a narrator explaining what the record means.

#### Character Critic
- **Stance:** Tests profile fidelity, relationship pressure, and knowledge basis.
- **Strengths:** Jinwoo remains evidence-first; Hyeok demands public procedure; Mujin guards exits; Taeseok interprets internal records; Elder protects institutional language.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The ensemble serves the raid without flattening the father mystery into a simple villain reveal.

#### Setting/Lore Expert
- **Stance:** Tests institutional logic, citeable facets, and bounded use of the bowl system.
- **Strengths:** All places are exact anchors; seals and ledgers have established legal authority; blood-contract mechanics remain held.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The sect feels dangerous because its bureaucracy can redirect force and manufacture family evidence.
