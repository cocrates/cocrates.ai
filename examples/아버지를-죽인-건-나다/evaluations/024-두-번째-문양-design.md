## Design Critique (required personas)

#### Target Reader
- **Stance:** Adult male regression-martial-arts reader seeking tactical evidence reversals, revenge pressure, and family secrets.
- **Strengths:** The second mark becomes a direct threat to Jinwoo’s interpretation of Dohyun, and the death-date statement is a strong click-forward hook.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The reader receives a concrete escalation without losing the revenge engine.

#### Genre Critic
- **Stance:** Tests whether the episode pays off the prior clue with earned advantage rather than a sudden lore dump.
- **Strengths:** Jinwoo earns the new inference by comparing object, record, and body; the father remains a dangerous unresolved target.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The genre promise of tactical reversal and family betrayal is strengthened.

#### Plot Expert
- **Stance:** Tests Hook alignment, causality, and Out scope.
- **Strengths:** Mark → limited seal possibility → Dohyun hand → death-date statement is causal, and every Hook surface carries the same claim.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The ending creates one clean next-episode obligation rather than a crowded mystery stack.

#### Reader-Editor
- **Stance:** Tests mobile rhythm, scene differentiation, and sellable closure.
- **Strengths:** Each scene changes the evidence subject from bowl to hand to direct speech; the final physical action is easy to scan and visualize.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** There is no procedural stall; the reader is rewarded with a personal cliffhanger.

#### Literary Critic
- **Stance:** Tests motif, restraint, and whether ambiguity is dramatized rather than explained.
- **Strengths:** The broken end and hand/bowl pairing carry the moral uncertainty through objects and contact.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The reader can infer competing meanings without receiving a thematic lecture.

#### Character Critic
- **Stance:** Tests motivation, voice, and profile-backed knowledge.
- **Strengths:** Jinwoo’s refusal to touch the evidence, Hyeok’s procedural brake, Taeseok’s bounded speech, and Dohyun’s evasive low voice are distinct and motivated.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** Jinwoo’s possible protective impulse remains a tactical problem, not an unexplained emotional reversal.

#### Setting/Lore Expert
- **Stance:** Tests catalog integrity and whether blood-vessel/medicine information stays within established rules.
- **Strengths:** The design uses only existing mark, seal, and dual-function rules; exact formula and full contract remain held.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The world expands through evidence while preserving credibility and mystery.
