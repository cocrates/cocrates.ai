## Design Critique
#### Target Reader
- Stance: Adult male regression/murim reader seeking father reversal and tactical betrayal detection.
- Strengths: The insult becomes a verifiable accusation, then a list-based ally threat.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Strong desire to identify the hidden helper without losing the father mystery.

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: Tests revenge-murim promise and delayed reveal discipline.
- Strengths: Jinwoo chooses evidence and leverage instead of an emotional outburst.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Delivers a strategic serial payoff while preserving the larger conspiracy.

#### Plot Expert
- Stance: Tests causality and Hook scope.
- Strengths: Eligibility condition causes evidence test; evidence test causes list reveal; list reveal matches 060’s alliance-betrayal direction.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Clear escalation with no premature identity answer.

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: Tests scene economy and Out density.
- Strengths: Three scenes each change access and information control; final obligation is singular.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The episode is a complete witness negotiation and a clean click-through.

#### Literary Critic
- Stance: Tests motifs and restraint.
- Strengths: Key, empty positions, and spreading red ink externalize permission, absence, and compromised trust.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The father question gains moral pressure without a thematic lecture.

#### Character Critic
- Stance: Tests motives, voices, and profile-backed knowledge.
- Strengths: Jinwoo refuses to guess the helper; Dohyun’s attempt to stop the key preserves guarded guilt; gatekeeper’s knowledge is profile-supported.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Each character makes a distinct choice under the same pressure.

#### Setting/Lore Expert
- Stance: Tests ritual evidence and physical rule consistency.
- Strengths: The episode reuses the locked facets and turns the two clamps/partial seal into causal evidence, not invented lore.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The old ritual feels materially present and investigable.
