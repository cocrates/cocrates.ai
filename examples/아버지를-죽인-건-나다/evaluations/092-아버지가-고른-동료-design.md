## Design Critique (required personas)
#### Target Reader
- **Stance:** Wants the father’s choice tested quickly and a stronger enemy design revealed.
- **Strengths:** The suspect is active rather than passively explained; the successor reason is a satisfying but incomplete reveal.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** High continuation pull; the next episode has a usable strategic consequence.

#### Genre Critic
- **Stance:** Tests 회귀 무협 strategy and conspiracy escalation.
- **Strengths:** Evidence beats prophecy; the enemy’s successor plan reframes prior betrayal without absolving the master.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** Delivers a reversal while preserving the infiltration objective.

#### Plot Expert
- **Stance:** Checks causal chain, Hook body alignment, and scope.
- **Strengths:** All Hook surfaces now carry the same reason; the Out does not execute the successor bait; physical markers make the testimony testable.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The revelation is earned and points cleanly to 093.

#### Reader-Editor
- **Stance:** Checks serialized density and information release.
- **Strengths:** The episode delays the key phrase until Scene 2 and corroborates it in Scene 3; no crowded Out.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** Low skim risk because each scene changes who controls the evidence.

#### Character Critic
- **Stance:** Checks motivation and profile-backed knowledge.
- **Strengths:** Dohyun’s selection follows his relationship with the disciple; the disciple’s knowledge follows his Core Drive; Jinwoo does not forgive the master by proxy.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The father’s trust is made costly rather than sentimental.

#### Literary Critic
- **Stance:** Checks motifs, image, and restraint.
- **Strengths:** Knife-sheath scratches embody self-censorship; bamboo and record board distinguish evidence from testimony; red thread gives the closing image physical tension.
- **Defects:** Motif placement is episode-level rather than explicit optional scene fields.
- **Reader impact:** Low risk; carry the motif placements into prose without explaining them.
