## Design Critique (required personas)

#### Target Reader
- Stance: Adult male-oriented regression-wuxia reader seeking revenge payoff, tactical procedure, and father-son reversal without sentimental absolution.
- Strengths: The long-held regression-cost reveal lands inside the public testimony framework; Jinwoo’s refusal to forgive preserves the revenge contract; the thrown-blade Out is immediate and visual.
- Defects: Low — Scene 2 carries several linked disclosures, so prose must keep each admission attached to a physical record or reaction rather than explaining the entire history in one uninterrupted speech.
- Reader impact: The risk is skimmed exposition; the design already counters it with blood, missing memory, record columns, and Lord’s active rebuttal.

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: Tests the design against regression-wuxia and revenge-serial promises.
- Strengths: A delayed truth payoff, public humiliation reversal, institutional evidence, and immediate combat escalation all satisfy genre expectations without a training detour.
- Defects: Low — “회귀의 대가” could become a familiar sacrifice reveal if the manuscript states it as noble; preserve the dual reading of protection and control.
- Reader impact: Keep the father’s confession morally contaminated so the reader receives catharsis without losing the revenge engine.

#### Plot Expert
- Stance: Tests causality, Hook alignment, and Out scope.
- Strengths: Scene transitions are causal; the Hook is identical in Summary, Out, Arc close, Seed, and final Turn/Transition; the blade attack follows the loss of contract interpretive control.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: —

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: Tests serialization, information density, and closing sell.
- Strengths: Scene 1 reopens the exact prior cliffhanger, Scene 3 prevents a premature emotional resolution, and Scene 4 gives a single clean next-episode obligation.
- Defects: Med — Scene 2 has eight outline beats and four dialogue units; in prose, rotate testimony with Jinwoo’s evidence checks and Lord’s interruption to avoid a lecture block.
- Reader impact: Apply as a generation constraint; a reader who came for the reveal must feel the truth being fought over, not merely announced.

#### Literary Critic
- Stance: Tests motif, restraint, and emotional meaning at design scope.
- Strengths: Empty memory spaces and the record/blade pairing make responsibility material; the closing image externalizes the failure of procedure.
- Defects: Low — Motif placement is episode-level rather than attached with explicit scene Motif touch fields; preserve both motifs during generation.
- Reader impact: Carry the motif cues without adding a thematic closing monologue.

#### Character Critic
- Stance: Tests profile-backed knowledge, motive, and relationship pressure.
- Strengths: Dohyun’s confession follows his established drive and old-dongmun conflict; Jinwoo’s evidence-first response matches his arc; Lord’s attack follows his control motive; Hyeok and the Elder Representative retain distinct institutional roles.
- Defects: Med — Jinwoo’s “truth does not erase revenge” stance must be shown as a costly refusal to simplify his father, not as a repeated slogan; give the decision a concrete record action.
- Reader impact: Apply in generation: make Jinwoo add or preserve a guilt entry rather than merely announce anger, so the reader experiences the separation of understanding and absolution.
