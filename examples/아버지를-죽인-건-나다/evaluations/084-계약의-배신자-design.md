## Design Critique (required personas)

#### Target Reader
- Stance: Adult male-oriented regression/murim web-novel reader seeking tactical reversals, father-son suspicion, and a sharp next-episode payoff.
- Strengths: The capture immediately rewards the 083 cliffhanger; the interrogation converts hidden family guilt into a concrete contract procedure; the awakening hook has clear serial pull.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The reader can track what changed and still has a specific unanswered question about what awakening will cost.

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: Tests the design against regression-murim revenge and cathartic confrontation contracts.
- Strengths: Jinwoo wins control without prematurely defeating the organization; the messenger remains a condition-delivery antagonist rather than a disposable fight.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The episode delivers tactical satisfaction while preserving the larger enemy threat.

#### Plot Expert
- Stance: Checks causality, escalation, Hook body alignment, and Hook scope.
- Strengths: 083 intrusion → capture → procedural interrogation → self-collateral reveal → awakening hook is clean; Summary, Out, Seed, and closing Turn match the series Hook.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: No causal gap forces the reader to accept an unearned revelation; each answer creates the next pressure.

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: Checks serial readability, exposition restraint, and closing density.
- Strengths: The closing carries one dominant obligation, not a stack of unrelated reveals; objects and body reactions keep dialogue scenes mobile.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Low skim risk for the target platform reader; the episode ends before the mechanism becomes a lecture.

#### Literary Critic
- Stance: Checks motif, sensory-emotional design, restraint, and image-based closure.
- Strengths: Red thread and split seal externalize control and broken authority; the closing image lets the body answer before the narration explains.
- Defects: Motifs are episode-planned rather than attached with explicit scene-level Motif touch fields.
- Reader impact: This is a Low craft-hand-off note, not a design barrier; the motifs are still placeable from the episode table and scene cues.

#### Character Critic
- Stance: Checks motive pressure, distinct voices, and profile-backed knowledge.
- Strengths: Jinwoo separates protection from exoneration; Dohyun’s attempt to stop the question preserves his established silence; the messenger’s limited authority matches his profile.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The father remains morally accountable, which protects the core revenge premise while deepening the relationship.

#### Setting/Lore Expert
- Stance: Checks contract rules, location anchors, staging continuity, and exposition load.
- Strengths: All locations and facets are catalogued; the new staging fixes capture/interrogation blocking; existing contract reactions are used without adding a new law.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The reader receives enough rule clarity to understand danger without being asked to memorize the full contract.
