## Design Critique (required personas)

#### Target Reader
- **Stance:** Adult male-oriented regression martial-arts web-novel reader seeking immediate advantage, rivalry pressure, and a sharp next-episode destination.
- **Strengths:** The duel hook pays forward through a physical verification and produces a concrete alliance condition.
- **Defects:** — after revision; Scene 2 now changes the threshold geometry through the sword exchange and document repositioning.
- **Reader impact:** The middle no longer depends on prose alone to distinguish two adjacent scenes at the same facet.

#### Genre Critic
- **Stance:** Tests regression martial-arts, sect-power, revenge, and male-oriented serial conventions.
- **Strengths:** Physical confrontation, evidence-based reversal, public status restoration, and forward pursuit hook all remain present.
- **Defects:** Low — carry the restraint that each material discrepancy should trigger the next; do not explain the full forgery scheme or culprit.
- **Reader impact:** The reversal remains earned by close observation rather than future-knowledge magic.

#### Plot Expert
- **Stance:** Checks causality, Episode List fidelity, prior-hook pickup, Hook alignment, and Hook scope.
- **Strengths:** Duel demand creates verification bargain; verification creates record leverage; leverage creates joint pursuit; the final destination exactly matches the Series Hook.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** One immediate question resolves and one bounded destination question opens.

#### Reader-Editor
- **Stance:** Checks serialization readability, exposition restraint, Out density, and closing sell.
- **Strengths:** The Out has one dominant obligation. The revised Scene 3 makes the faint seal a single image rather than a competing announcement.
- **Defects:** — after revision; departure preparation supports the destination rather than becoming an independent hook.
- **Reader impact:** The final click is directed toward one place and one unresolved meaning.

#### Literary Critic
- **Stance:** Checks motif, sensory image, restraint, and whether meaning is dramatized.
- **Strengths:** Threshold, sheath, seal, and public record form a coherent image system; Scene 3 now has an explicit motif touch.
- **Defects:** Low — preserve the seal-mark image without explaining its symbolism.
- **Reader impact:** Object-based continuity gives the alliance scene a distinctive tactile identity.

#### Character Critic
- **Stance:** Checks motivation, profile-backed recognition, voice, and relationship movement.
- **Strengths:** 진우’s records-first behavior, 혁’s honor pressure, and 무진’s procedural control remain distinct.
- **Defects:** Low — retain 혁’s public cost: he attaches 남궁가’s name to the joint pursuit, so acceptance is chosen responsibility rather than sudden trust.
- **Reader impact:** Rivalry-to-alliance movement remains credible and conditional.
