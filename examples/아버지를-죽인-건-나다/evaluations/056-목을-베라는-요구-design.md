## Design Critique (required personas)

#### Target Reader
- Stance: Adult male web-novel reader seeking regression advantage, faction maneuvering, revenge catharsis, and father-son reversals.
- Strengths: Immediate opening question, tactical reversal, and a strong black-blood serial hook.
- Defects: — after staging and spatial handoff were applied.
- Reader impact: The reader can now reconstruct why the messenger misses Dohyun’s survival and why the blade still costs him.

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: Tests regression-murim and revenge web-novel contracts.
- Strengths: Future knowledge is leverage, not prophecy; fake execution is tied to surveillance and filial revenge.
- Defects: —; the sightline and messenger verification are now locked in staging.
- Reader impact: Tactical satisfaction is physically credible.

#### Plot Expert
- Stance: Causality, hook alignment, and scope control.
- Strengths: Summary maps cleanly to Scenes 1–3; Out and Turn preserve one black-blood reveal; no scope creep.
- Defects: — after the added below-platform facet and staging make the handoff explicit.
- Reader impact: The final turn follows causally from the false execution rather than feeling like a new scene invention.

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: Serialization rhythm, scene separation, and closing density.
- Strengths: Accept → endanger → evidence is clean; one principal Out obligation; platform/below-platform separation is now visible.
- Defects: —.
- Reader impact: Lower skim risk at the episode’s climax.

#### Literary Critic
- Stance: Motif, image, restraint, and emotional afterimage.
- Strengths: The blade motif moves from legal performance to bodily harm; black blood materializes hidden cost without explanation.
- Defects: Low carry constraint only: do not add explanatory diagnosis or Dohyun’s inner motive in prose.
- Reader impact: Ambiguity and the closing image invite the next episode.

#### Character Critic
- Stance: Motivation, profile-backed behavior, and relationship pressure.
- Strengths: Jinwoo’s reversal fits his drive; Dohyun’s physical blade-push extends his established judgment through action; Hyuk protects public evidence.
- Defects: Low carry constraint only: show Dohyun’s pressure physically, without paternal explanation.
- Reader impact: Dohyun remains morally unreadable and the father-son conflict stays alive.
