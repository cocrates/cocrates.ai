## Design Critique (required personas)

#### Target Reader
- Stance: Adult male-oriented revenge-wuxia reader seeking action payoff and morally hard father-son decisions.
- Strengths: The last-blade cliff is paid immediately; the duel does not erase Jinwoo’s guilt; the Out promises a meaningful internal choice with external combat consequences.
- Defects: Low — The memory overlay must remain tactile and conflict-driven rather than becoming a long flashback lecture.
- Reader impact: Carry as a prose constraint; the target reader should feel the past invading the duel, not leave the action for exposition.

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: Tests revenge, regression, and final-duel genre contracts.
- Strengths: Defense, antagonist provocation, seal danger, and a delayed moral-action Hook all fit the genre promise.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: —

#### Plot Expert
- Stance: Tests causality and Hook alignment/scope.
- Strengths: Every scene follows from the prior physical state; all Hook surfaces carry one observable acceptance claim; no Episode 116 payoff is stolen.
- Defects: — (Hook alignment and scope checked; no hit)
- Reader impact: —

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: Tests serialization, closing density, and action-to-memory rhythm.
- Strengths: The final Transition has one dominant obligation and the 7,900 forecast leaves enough room for action without a new subplot.
- Defects: Med — Scenes 2–4 contain multiple blood-mark, memory, and anger signals; prose must give each a new power shift rather than repeat “봉인이 되살아난다.”
- Reader impact: Apply as Carry-⑥ so the target reader experiences escalation, not repetition.

#### Literary Critic
- Stance: Tests motif and image discipline.
- Strengths: The hand/body motif makes responsibility physical; the overlapping blade marks give the moral choice a concrete closing image.
- Defects: Low — Episode-level motifs lack explicit scene Motif-touch fields; preserve their placements during generation.
- Reader impact: Carry-⑥; no design rewrite needed because each scene already contains the physical cue.

#### Character Critic
- Stance: Tests profile-backed action, voice, and relationship pressure.
- Strengths: Jinwoo’s refusal to use either denial or rage follows his responsibility arc; Dohyun does not demand absolution; Lord’s teacher-like coercion follows his control motive.
- Defects: — (profile-backed motive and voice checks pass)
- Reader impact: —

No Setting/Lore Expert or Literary Awards Juror is required: this is not Episode 001 or a major new lore debut, and overview.md has no prestige criterion.
