## Design Critique (required personas)

#### Target Reader
- Stance: adult male-oriented regression wuxia reader seeking revenge, evidence reversals, and father-son pressure.
- Strengths: the death evidence becomes a ritual-made object, while the father confrontation makes the reveal personal.
- Defects: exact max forecast could make a manuscript overrun if every exchange is expanded → Low → keep generation lean and cut repeated confirmations.
- Reader impact: the bodily consequence keeps the evidence work from feeling like passive archive research.

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: checked the regression/wuxia contract and earned reversal.
- Strengths: ritual evidence, black blood, and a forbidden condition deliver genre-specific escalation.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: readers receive a stronger threat rather than a purely explanatory reveal.

#### Plot Expert
- Stance: checked causality, Hook alignment, and Hook scope.
- Strengths: the date leads to classification, then ritual record, then third-condition reaction; the Hook is neither softened nor hardened.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: the next question is causally forced, not artificially appended.

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: checked scene transitions, exposition, and closing density.
- Strengths: the last scene carries one dominant obligation and holds treatment/mechanism back.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: a single next-step question remains visible despite several evidence layers.

#### Literary Critic
- Stance: checked motifs, sensory pairing, and image-led close.
- Strengths: the blank recipient and finger motif translate abstract culpability into objects/body.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: the ending can land through the four-word record rather than thematic explanation.

#### Character Critic
- Stance: checked agency, voice, and profile-backed knowledge.
- Strengths: 진우 verifies instead of forgiving; 도현’s body carries the cost of silence; 혁 and 의원의 제자는 distinct evidence roles.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: the father-son conflict advances through incompatible permissions to speak.
