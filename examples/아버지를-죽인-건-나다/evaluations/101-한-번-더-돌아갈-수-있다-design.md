## Design Critique (required personas)

#### Target Reader
- Stance: Strong continuation for adult male-oriented regression martial-arts readers.
- Strengths: The record hook is converted into a choice instead of a gimmick; mother becomes an active actor; the final 부탁 is a sharp serial pull.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The reader will continue to learn whether this is protection, coercion, or another engineered trap.

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: The episode delivers a confrontation and reverses the expected “use regression to fix everything” move.
- Strengths: Refusal has a cost; the mother’s choice prevents a simple rescue victory.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The genre promise of family reversal and tactical consequence is strengthened.

#### Plot Expert
- Stance: The causal chain and Hook body are aligned.
- Strengths: The prior record leads directly to the refusal, seal test, and exact 부탁; no Out creep.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The reader understands what changes now and what must be answered next.

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: Compact, sellable confrontation episode.
- Strengths: Each scene changes the decision state; the ending has one dominant obligation.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Low skim risk and high click-through pressure.

#### Literary Critic
- Stance: The design keeps the theme in physical objects and withheld explanation.
- Strengths: Folded record and bound wrist create a coherent visual-emotional structure.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The family conflict can deepen without a moral speech.
