## Design Critique (required personas)

#### Target Reader
- **Stance:** Adult male-oriented regression martial-arts reader seeking tactical advantage, rivalry pressure, family mystery, and a sharp cliffhanger.
- **Strengths:** Converts the prior name reveal into an actionable technique and a live bait operation; the `완성품` label is personal and concrete.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The episode gives both a tactical win and a more disturbing classification, sustaining next-click pressure.

#### Genre Critic
- **Stance:** Tests regression-martial-arts promise, evidence-based advantage, action pressure, and payoff.
- **Strengths:** Jinwoo uses a discovered formula as a counter-tracking weapon rather than receiving an unexplained power-up; the enemy contact is earned through the bait.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The genre reader receives a clever reversal and an enemy escalation without a training detour.

#### Plot Expert
- **Stance:** Checks causality, pacing, Hook alignment, and Hook scope.
- **Strengths:** Each turn follows materially: partial seal → formula response → reversed signal → tracker label. Summary, Out, Seeds, and closing Turn all match the series Hook.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The reader can understand why the tracker arrives and why the label matters without receiving its full meaning.

#### Reader-Editor
- **Stance:** Checks mobile serialization rhythm, exposition restraint, scene handoffs, and closing density.
- **Strengths:** Every scene ends with a physical handoff; the final scene has one principal hook rather than a stack of unrelated reveals.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The design supports fast reading while giving each scene a distinct evidence stage.

#### Literary Critic
- **Stance:** Checks motif, image, restraint, and reader-discovered meaning.
- **Strengths:** Breath, ink, blood, and the wrist line make the hidden record bodily without explaining the theme; the closing image is concrete.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The mystery gains unease through material recurrence rather than authorial explanation.

#### Character Critic
- **Stance:** Checks motivation, distinct voices, alliance pressure, and profile-backed recognition.
- **Strengths:** Jinwoo’s restraint, Hyeok’s direct risk challenge, Mujin’s operational eye, and the tracker’s limited label are distinct and profile-supported.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The alliance remains credible because both partners contest the bait while still carrying out bounded roles.

#### Setting/Lore Expert
- **Stance:** Checks location facets, world rules, catalog integrity, and Hold discipline.
- **Strengths:** All three old-battlefield facets are exact anchors; bloodline reaction and tracking marks are observed rather than over-systematized; the tracker is catalogued before citation.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The world feels rule-bound and dangerous while the episode remains event-led.
