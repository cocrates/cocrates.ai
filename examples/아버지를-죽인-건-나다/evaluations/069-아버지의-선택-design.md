## Design Critique (required personas)
#### Target Reader
- Stance: Adult male-oriented regression-murim reader seeking immediate payoff, bodily cost, and betrayal reversals.
- Strengths: The previous cliffhanger pays into a limited cut, then escalates into a sect-wide crisis and a strong master reveal.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The episode supplies two click-throughs—“what did the cut cost?” and “how is the dead master leading the coup?”—without crowding the Out.

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: Tests regression-murim and internal-coup serial contracts.
- Strengths: Limited victory with immediate cost, simultaneous uprising, and a supposedly dead mentor reversal are genre-appropriate and earned by prior traces.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The episode raises action scale without turning the protagonist’s choice into an effortless win.

#### Plot Expert
- Stance: Audits causality, Hook body alignment, and scope.
- Strengths: Cut → tracking → uprising → sword-trace identity is causal; all Hook surfaces name one master identity claim; no direct 070 scene is stolen.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: A cold reader can follow why the personal cut activates the wider crisis and why the master conclusion is evidence-based.

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: Checks serialization, Out density, and event readability.
- Strengths: Each scene has a distinct location/function; the final Out has one identity obligation, not a stacked chase/arrival/attack.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Strong next-episode pull with manageable information load.

#### Literary Critic
- Stance: Checks motifs, sensory-emotional craft, and restraint.
- Strengths: The cut fragment and displaced sword-tip motif turn a moral choice into physical images; the master remains a trace rather than a speech.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The reversal is felt through material evidence rather than explained theme.

#### Character Critic
- Stance: Checks agency, motivation, voice, and profile-backed recognition.
- Strengths: Jinwoo chooses a limited cut; Dohyun’s protection carries cost; Hyuk verifies; the child blocks substitution; the master identity uses profile-backed habits.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Every major relationship exerts pressure without taking the choice away from Jinwoo.

#### Setting/Lore Expert
- Stance: Checks blood-contract rule fidelity, location facets, and named-entity discipline.
- Strengths: All facets are exact anchors; unnamed coup movement remains ambient; the tracking effect is already established in the world bible.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The scale expands from shrine to sect without an uncatalogued rule or crowd exposition dump.
