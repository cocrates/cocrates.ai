## Design Critique (required personas)

#### Target Reader
- **Stance:** Adult male regression-martial-arts reader seeking tactical reversal, sect control, and ambiguous father conflict.
- **Strengths:** The letter makes the father conflict operational and then reverses the apparent external threat into an internal target.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The reader has both an immediate condition and a concrete next antagonist direction.

#### Genre Critic
- **Stance:** Tests revenge-serial promise, martial-arts evidence logic, and earned escalation.
- **Strengths:** Jinwoo wins through seals and ledgers rather than a power-up; the threat becomes a sect-control problem.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The episode supplies tactical satisfaction without resolving the larger conspiracy too early.

#### Plot Expert
- **Stance:** Checks causality, Summary/Hook alignment, and Out scope.
- **Strengths:** Condition → delivery trace → elder seal is a clean causal chain; body Hook surfaces match exactly.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The next episode promise is legible and earned.

#### Reader-Editor
- **Stance:** Checks mobile rhythm, procedural drag, and closing density.
- **Strengths:** Each scene changes the evidence state; the closing has one primary reveal.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The investigation reads as escalating action, not a static document review.

#### Literary Critic
- **Stance:** Checks material motifs, restraint, and closing image.
- **Strengths:** Hands, seals, fibers, wax, and the ballot table keep betrayal physical.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** Ambiguity is carried by objects and choices rather than exposition.

#### Character Critic
- **Stance:** Checks profile fidelity, motivation, and unsupported recognition.
- **Strengths:** Jinwoo follows evidence; Dohyun refuses explanation while retaining control of the sword; Hyeok and Mujin have distinct functions.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The family conflict remains emotionally charged without forcing Dohyun into a protective verdict.

#### Setting/Lore Expert
- **Stance:** Checks location anchors, institutional logic, and contract restraint.
- **Strengths:** Existing main-house facets, ledger authority, seal practice, and documented contract uncertainty are used without new rules.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The sect’s internal bureaucracy becomes a credible threat mechanism.
