- Defects: —

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: Checks the revenge-regression contract.

#### Plot Expert
- Stance: Checks causality, Hook alignment, and scope.

- Defects: —

- Stance: Checks serialization and exposition restraint.

- Defects: —
