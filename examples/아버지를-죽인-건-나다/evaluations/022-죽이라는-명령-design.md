## Design Critique (required personas)

#### Target Reader
- **Stance:** Adult male regression-martial-arts reader seeking tactical evidence reversals and family betrayal pressure.
- **Strengths:** The father record is not defused; it becomes a live investigation involving a familiar silent retainer.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The reader receives a concrete answer-shaped clue without losing the click-forward mystery.

#### Genre Critic
- **Stance:** Tests serial revenge escalation and earned investigative advantage.
- **Strengths:** Jinwoo wins through material comparison and pressure, not a sudden power upgrade.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The genre promise of tactical reversal remains active while the family threat deepens.

#### Plot Expert
- **Stance:** Tests causality, Hook alignment, and scope.
- **Strengths:** The 021 record causes the Gahwan questioning; Gahwan’s retreat causes the room search; the search produces the Hook.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The episode has one clean evidence chain rather than disconnected clues.

#### Reader-Editor
- **Stance:** Tests mobile rhythm, procedural drag, and serialization closure.
- **Strengths:** Each scene changes the object of pursuit: witness → room → bowl/handwriting.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The investigation remains event-driven and ends on a concrete reason to open Episode 023.

#### Literary Critic
- **Stance:** Tests material motif, restraint, and reader inference.
- **Strengths:** Teacup, ink, paper, and bowl translate silence into physical evidence; the design avoids a thematic explanation.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The reader can feel the gap between resemblance and proof.

#### Character Critic
- **Stance:** Tests motivation, voice, and profile-backed knowledge.
- **Strengths:** Gahwan’s established evasive speech and teacup habit become behavior under pressure; Jinwoo refuses premature certainty; Hyeok and Taeseok retain distinct functions.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** Gahwan becomes suspicious without being flattened into a confirmed traitor.

#### Setting/Lore Expert
- **Stance:** Tests document logic, bowl evidence, and catalog integrity.
- **Strengths:** All facets and staging paths exist; bowl marks remain bounded evidence; no contract rule is invented.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The sect’s archive and medicine systems feel legible without an exposition dump.
