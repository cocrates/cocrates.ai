## Design Critique (required personas)

#### Target Reader
- **Stance:** Adult male regression-martial-arts reader seeking tactical reversals and father-centered revenge pressure.
- **Strengths:** A death date is a sharper weapon than another vague mark; the one-year discrepancy is easy to understand and click forward.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The episode turns the father mystery into a countdown without resolving it.

#### Genre Critic
- **Stance:** Tests whether the date disclosure creates earned regression tension rather than prophecy exposition.
- **Strengths:** Jinwoo’s hidden comparison makes the future unreliable while preserving his tactical edge.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The genre promise of changing a known future is directly activated.

#### Plot Expert
- **Stance:** Tests Hook alignment, causality, and Out scope.
- **Strengths:** Wrist statement → written date → hidden one-year comparison → reason withheld is clean and causal.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** One measurable discrepancy replaces a crowded stack of mysteries.

#### Reader-Editor
- **Stance:** Tests mobile rhythm and closure.
- **Strengths:** Each scene has a new information operation: state, compare, interrogate; the blank ledger margin is visually clear.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The episode is easy to follow while preserving a strong next-click question.

#### Literary Critic
- **Stance:** Tests motif and restraint.
- **Strengths:** Written date and blank reason give the abstract future a physical object and a visible absence.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** Ambiguity is material rather than explanatory.

#### Character Critic
- **Stance:** Tests motivation, voice, and relationship pressure.
- **Strengths:** Jinwoo’s act of hiding the one-year difference embodies his fear of exposing the regression; Dohyun’s refusal fits his established protective secrecy.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The father-son conflict deepens without an unsupported confession.

#### Setting/Lore Expert
- **Stance:** Tests date records, memory variation, and world-rule restraint.
- **Strengths:** No new calendar, time law, or contract mechanism is invented; the discrepancy is a POV observation.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The altered future feels rule-bound and legible.
