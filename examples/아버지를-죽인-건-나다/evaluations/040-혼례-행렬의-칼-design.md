## Design Critique (required personas)
#### Target Reader
- Stance: Adult male web-novel reader seeking regression advantage, faction tactics, martial action, and family-revenge escalation.
- Strengths: The 039 promise pays off immediately; the dual-target document gives the attack a tactical rather than random purpose; the public alliance proposal creates a strong serial turn.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The reader can track what is at risk and why the next episode must change the rules of the Jinwoo–Hyuk relationship.

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: Tests the regression martial serial contract and action readability.
- Strengths: Secret-meeting payoff, ambush, evidence seizure, and faction-level counterplay all land inside one episode.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Delivers the expected satisfying reversal without making future knowledge omniscient.

#### Plot Expert
- Stance: Checks causality, Hook alignment, and Hook scope.
- Strengths: 039 note → meeting → target document → attack → evidence → public proposal is a clean causal chain; Out matches the series Hook exactly.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: No unexplained jump or extra Out obligation should make the next episode feel earned.

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: Checks serialization, exposition restraint, and closing density.
- Strengths: The closing has one primary obligation—proposal—rather than stacking acceptance, signature, and backstory; the attack remains legible.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Strong page-turn rhythm with a clean final turn; low skim risk.

#### Literary Critic
- Stance: Checks motif and emotional meaning without rewarding explanation for its own sake.
- Strengths: Black shade/white stone externalizes hidden command versus public legitimacy; two insignia turn political ambiguity into a visible object.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The episode’s meaning can be discovered through the scene rather than announced.

#### Character Critic
- Stance: Checks drive, voice, and profile-backed knowledge.
- Strengths: 진우 protects evidence and people simultaneously; 혁’s public proposal grows from his established 명분 drive; internalist remains limited and self-protective rather than omniscient.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The alliance is a character choice under pressure, not a plot convenience.

#### Setting/Lore Expert
- Stance: Checks faction mechanics and citeable location integrity.
- Strengths: The new `혼례 행렬 길목` facet is explicitly catalogued; distributed 흑풍루 commands and transport marks are used without inventing a new rule.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The reader learns the setting through an actionable threat and can visualize the public/private contrast.
