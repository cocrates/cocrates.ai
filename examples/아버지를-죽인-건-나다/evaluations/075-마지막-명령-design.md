## Design Critique (required personas)
#### Target Reader
- Stance: Adult male web-novel reader seeking regression martial-arts strategy, family reversals, and cathartic pressure.
- Strengths: The mother’s command is converted into a mechanical clue rather than a sentimental absolution; the map gives a concrete next-episode target.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Immediate continuation pressure from both the father’s withheld cost and the four-mark map.

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: Tests regression martial-arts and revenge-serial promises.
- Strengths: Evidence-first confrontation, tactical verification, and a transaction-based cliffhanger satisfy the genre contract.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The episode delivers a win in understanding without prematurely defeating the enemy.

#### Plot Expert
- Stance: Tests causal order, prior-hook pickup, and Hook body alignment/scope.
- Strengths: Scene transitions are causal; Summary, Arc, Out, Seed, and closing Turn all carry the exact four-mark map claim.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The reader knows exactly what was learned and what Episode 076 must test.

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: Tests serialization, exposition restraint, and closing density.
- Strengths: Four scenes each have one job; the Out has one primary obligation—verify the false mark—with the contact condition as support.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Low skim risk; the repeated paper/map objects are functional rather than explanatory padding.

#### Literary Critic
- Stance: Tests motif, sensory pressure, and moral complexity at design scope.
- Strengths: Torn paper and folded map externalize incomplete protection and transactional information; closing avoids thematic speech.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The father is neither redeemed by revelation nor flattened as villain, preserving the series’ ethical tension.

#### Character Critic
- Stance: Tests motivation, profile-backed knowledge, and relationship pressure.
- Strengths: Jinwoo separates fact from pardon; Dohyun admits the protective mechanism but withholds the other child’s name under an established contract effect; Hyuk remains procedural; the internal자 stays a conditional survivor.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The family conflict intensifies because the new truth creates a harder question rather than ending the grievance.
