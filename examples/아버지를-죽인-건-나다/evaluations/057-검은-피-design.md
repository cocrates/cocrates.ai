## Design Critique (required personas)

#### Target Reader
- Stance: Adult male regression/murim reader seeking tactical revenge, family reversals, and concrete cliffhangers.
- Strengths: The black blood is immediately promoted from image to public weapon; the treatment condition personalizes the mystery; the matching mark is a strong Out.
- Defects: —; the three situations are spatially distinct and the Out has one dominant obligation.
- Reader impact: The reader wants the next episode to learn whether saving the father is equivalent to killing Jinwoo.

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: Regression-murim and revenge contract.
- Strengths: No healing shortcut; the protagonist’s tactical advantage collides with a cost he cannot outmaneuver by prediction.
- Defects: —.
- Reader impact: The episode delivers escalation rather than a simple diagnosis or cure.

#### Plot Expert
- Stance: Causality, hook alignment, and scope.
- Strengths: Episode 056 blood leads directly to public proof, condition, and matching mark; Hook strength is stable across body surfaces; no Out creep.
- Defects: —.
- Reader impact: Each reveal follows from the previous physical evidence.

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: Serialization rhythm, density, and closing conversion.
- Strengths: Public → private → public rhythm gives the episode varied pressure; the final image is compact and actionable.
- Defects: —.
- Reader impact: No procedural block should stall the mobile reader because every record changes the power balance.

#### Literary Critic
- Stance: Motif and restraint.
- Strengths: Black blood becomes a recurring material motif; the matching curve is an image, not a thematic lecture.
- Defects: Low carry: do not explain the moral meaning of the shared condition in prose.
- Reader impact: The reader discovers the father’s protection and violence may be the same structure.

#### Character Critic
- Stance: Motivation, voice, and profile-backed recognition.
- Strengths: Jinwoo’s refusal to hide evidence fits his drive; Dohyun speaks only to warn; Hyuk separates record from inference. The mark comparison is observable, not an unsupported identity revelation.
- Defects: —.
- Reader impact: Dohyun remains an active but unreadable source of danger and protection.
