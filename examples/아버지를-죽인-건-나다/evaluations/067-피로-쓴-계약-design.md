## Design Critique (required personas)
#### Target Reader
- Stance: Adult male-oriented regression-murim reader seeking bodily stakes, document reversals, and father-son mistrust.
- Strengths: The episode converts a paper clue into an immediate death condition and then into a nearby objective; the shrine location is a strong click-through Hook.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Clear escalation from “what does the clause mean?” to “the dangerous blood seal is at home.”

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: Tests the design against regression-murim and family-revenge serial contracts.
- Strengths: The protagonist investigates rather than passively receives lore; the blood-seal reveal is a genre-appropriate reversal without early action inflation.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The episode delivers a concrete mystery and preserves the next episode’s physical confrontation.

#### Plot Expert
- Stance: Audits causality, Hook alignment, and scope.
- Strengths: Original → reaction → record → storage location is legible; all Hook surfaces confirm one location claim; no Out creep.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: A cold reader can see why the group moves from the basement to the shrine and why opening it must wait.

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: Checks serial pacing, exposition restraint, and closing density.
- Strengths: Each scene changes evidence control; the final Transition has one dominant obligation, the shrine blood seal.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Low stall risk; the procedural work remains tied to life-or-death pressure.

#### Literary Critic
- Stance: Checks motif, sensory craft, and Hold discipline.
- Strengths: Blurred blood and closed altar make protection/ownership visible without a thematic speech; the final image is place-based.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The emotional ambiguity remains earned rather than explained.

#### Character Critic
- Stance: Checks profile-backed motivation and relationship pressure.
- Strengths: Jinwoo’s distrust drives every test; Dohyun’s refusal is constrained and active; Hyuk’s procedural role and the child’s autonomy prevent passive witnessing.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The contract mystery remains a father-son conflict, not detached lore.

#### Setting/Lore Expert
- Stance: Checks blood-contract rules and citeable shrine facets.
- Strengths: The design uses only existing blood-contract implications and exact anchors `사당 외실` / `혈인 보관단`; no new ritual law is introduced.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The reader learns the location and danger without receiving the full removal procedure too early.
