## Design Critique (required personas)

#### Target Reader
- **Stance:** Adult male regression-martial-arts reader seeking tactical evidence reversals, family betrayal pressure, and a click-forward hook.
- **Strengths:** Converts Gahwan’s escape into a material trail and gives the reader a visible contradiction in Jinwoo’s future memory.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The reader gets progress without a premature guilt verdict.

#### Genre Critic
- **Stance:** Tests revenge escalation, earned advantage, and genre promise.
- **Strengths:** Jinwoo advances through route control and forensic comparison rather than unexplained strength.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The family-mystery revenge engine remains active and concrete.

#### Plot Expert
- **Stance:** Tests causality, Hook body alignment, and Out scope.
- **Strengths:** Flight → room access → memory discrepancy is causal; all Hook surfaces carry one claim.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** No false resolution or crowded next-episode obligation interrupts the click-forward.

#### Reader-Editor
- **Stance:** Tests mobile rhythm, scene differentiation, and serialized closure.
- **Strengths:** The three scenes change evidence status in sequence; the closing image is easy to visualize.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** Readers can skim neither the middle nor the end without losing a new physical clue.

#### Literary Critic
- **Stance:** Tests motif, restraint, and whether the closing image carries meaning without explanation.
- **Strengths:** Empty objects and non-overlapping marks express the memory/current gap through material evidence.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** Ambiguity feels earned because the reader sees the discrepancy rather than being told its theme.

#### Character Critic
- **Stance:** Tests motivation, voice, and profile-backed knowledge.
- **Strengths:** Jinwoo refuses to convert suspicion into guilt; Hyeok’s public-limit voice and Taeseok’s technical boundary stay distinct.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** Jinwoo’s restraint reads as tactical control, not indecision.

#### Setting/Lore Expert
- **Stance:** Tests catalog integrity, bowl evidence, and bounded world rules.
- **Strengths:** All locations, facets, props, and staging files are cited; the bowl remains a clue rather than an unexplained diagnostic machine.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The sect’s records feel dangerous and legible without a lore dump.
