## Design Critique (required personas)

#### Target Reader
- Stance: Adult male regression/murim reader seeking a costly power-up and a tactical counterattack.
- Strengths: The cost is revealed before the choice, preventing a free awakening; the first-base hook is concrete.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The reader gets power-up anticipation, danger, and a clear target without losing the revenge premise.

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: Tests the costed-awakening and first-counterattack genre contract.
- Strengths: Partial release is a defensive tactical tool rather than a flashy new technique; the attack is chosen from evidence.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Satisfies escalation while preserving threat credibility.

#### Plot Expert
- Stance: Checks Hook alignment, causality, and scope.
- Strengths: 084 reaction → test → cost → attack decision is causal; the closing does not steal the base assault scheduled next.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The reader knows why the attack is happening and what it costs.

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: Checks serialization, exposition restraint, and closing density.
- Strengths: Each scene has one evidence/choice turn; the Out has one dominant obligation.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Low stall risk and high click-through clarity.

#### Literary Critic
- Stance: Checks motifs, image, and emotional restraint.
- Strengths: Shortened breath externalizes time cost; the bowl and thread preserve the series’ material motif system.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The power-up retains emotional weight without a thematic speech.

#### Character Critic
- Stance: Checks Jinwoo/Dohyun conflict and Hyuk’s procedural role.
- Strengths: Dohyun’s attempt to stop the release risks repeating his control; Jinwoo’s acceptance restores agency; Hyuk’s voice remains evidentiary.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The father-son conflict advances through a choice, not an exposition confession.

#### Setting/Lore Expert
- Stance: Checks seal/poison rules, facet integrity, and Hold discipline.
- Strengths: Existing world rules support the cost; all three facets are catalogued; the first base remains intentionally undescribed.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The system is legible enough for danger while preserving mystery.
