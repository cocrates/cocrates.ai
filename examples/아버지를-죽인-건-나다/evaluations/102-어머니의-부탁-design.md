## Design Critique (required personas)

#### Target Reader
- Stance: Adult male web-novel reader of regression martial arts, clan control, revenge, and family reversals.
- Strengths: The prior cliffhanger is paid immediately; the mother’s 부탁 becomes evidence rather than melodrama; Dohyun’s declaration is a strong serial pull.
- Defects: The 7,900 forecast leaves little headroom for prose overshoot, so Stage ⑥ must avoid adding a second reveal or extended explanation. Severity: Med. Proposed fix: Carry-⑥ length discipline and do not add motive exposition.
- Reader impact: The reader gets the desired confrontation without losing the mystery of who is manipulating whom.

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: Tests the regression martial-arts and revenge serial contract.
- Strengths: Prior knowledge functions as tactical verification, not omniscience; the family-choice trap produces a concrete confrontation; the ending points directly to a father-son fight.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The episode delivers escalation and a clean next-episode promise without resolving the final boss early.

#### Plot Expert
- Stance: Tests causality, Hook alignment, and scope.
- Strengths: Episode 101 부탁 → structural comparison → blood-triggered collision → Dohyun memory recovery → declaration is causal; Summary, Out, Seeds, and closing Turn match at one evidence strength; no Out creep.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The reader can understand why the declaration happens now while still needing Episode 103 for the reason.

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: Tests serialization, density, and closing sell.
- Strengths: Four distinct turns prevent a single-room conversation from flattening; physical responses carry exposition; the final image is readable and not overcrowded.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The episode has a clear page-turn rhythm and a single dominant reason to continue.

#### Literary Critic
- Stance: Tests motif, sensory integration, and restraint.
- Strengths: Repeated condition language is materialized through paper placement and blood lines; the returning voice turns memory into responsibility rather than sentimental reunion; the closing image avoids thematic speech.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Emotional weight comes from withheld explanation and physical choice, preserving the work’s cold tone.

#### Character Critic
- Stance: Tests profile-backed knowledge, motivation, and voice.
- Strengths: Jinwoo’s habit of checking hands and seals drives the investigation; mother acts from her established goal of preserving his agency; Lord uses his established educational contract voice; Dohyun’s recognition is grounded in the established father-son relationship and the mother’s childhood address.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Each family member acts from a recognizable prior drive rather than serving only as a plot device.
