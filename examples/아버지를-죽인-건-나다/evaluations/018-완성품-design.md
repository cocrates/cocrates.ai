## Design Critique (required personas)

#### Target Reader
- **Stance:** Adult male regression-martial-arts reader seeking tactical wins, personal mystery, and father-centered reversals.
- **Strengths:** The `완성품` cliffhanger pays off in action, then becomes childhood surveillance evidence and a direct father command.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The episode supplies a clean victory while making the victory personally disturbing, increasing next-click pressure.

#### Genre Critic
- **Stance:** Tests regression-martial-arts contract, power logic, confrontation, and payoff timing.
- **Strengths:** Jinwoo wins through bounded reaction control and teamwork, not a sudden complete technique unlock.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The reader receives a tactical reversal and a credible escalation without a training detour.

#### Plot Expert
- **Stance:** Checks causality, pacing, Hook alignment, and Hook scope.
- **Strengths:** Bait encounter causes the fight; fight produces evidence; evidence causes the Dohyun confrontation; all body Hook surfaces match the departure command.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The episode’s forward motion is legible and the closing command is earned rather than arbitrary.

#### Reader-Editor
- **Stance:** Checks serialization rhythm, exposition restraint, transitions, and Out density.
- **Strengths:** Each scene has one job and hands a physical object or unresolved pressure to the next; the Out has one primary obligation.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** Mobile readers get a fight opening, a compact evidence reveal, and a clean cliffhanger without a crowded final paragraph.

#### Literary Critic
- **Stance:** Checks motif, image, restraint, and emotional subtext.
- **Strengths:** The wrist line and the hand that removes the body turn invisible history into physical recurring images; Dohyun’s motive remains unreadable.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The family mystery deepens through action and objects rather than explanatory sentiment.

#### Character Critic
- **Stance:** Checks motivation, voice, alliance pressure, and profile-backed knowledge.
- **Strengths:** Jinwoo separates evidence from inference; Hyeok pushes public accountability; Mujin preserves procedure; Dohyun acts without inner disclosure.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The allies remain credible and Jinwoo’s suspicion grows from a visible choice, not a forced misunderstanding.

#### Setting/Lore Expert
- **Stance:** Checks world consistency, catalog integrity, facets, and Hold discipline.
- **Strengths:** All location facets are exact; bloodline reaction remains an observed mechanism; surveillance is not inflated into a new complete system.
- **Defects:** —
- **Reader impact:** The world feels rule-bound while the mystery remains open.
