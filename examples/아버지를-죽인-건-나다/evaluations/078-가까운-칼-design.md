## Design Critique
#### Target Reader
- Stance: Adult male regression/martial web-novel reader seeking tactical reversals and revenge pressure.
- Strengths: Turns a suspected ally into a witness and then turns the witness into a mirror of Jinwoo’s own violence.
- Defects: The 7,900-char forecast leaves little manuscript headroom; generation must stay lean. Low, operational.
- Reader impact: The strong 079 hook outweighs the minor headroom risk if prose avoids confirmation loops.

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: Tests regression mystery and revenge contract.
- Strengths: Residue test produces an earned memory reveal, not a convenient confession.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Genre satisfaction comes from a reversal of the protagonist’s certainty.

#### Plot Expert
- Stance: Tests Hook body alignment, causality, and scope.
- Strengths: The memory fragment is bounded; motive and original evidence remain held; no Out creep.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Reader gets an answer-shaped image while the central why remains alive.

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: Tests serial density and closing click-through.
- Strengths: Four distinct evidence jobs and a physical final image.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The oath creates a clean next-episode question.

#### Literary Critic
- Stance: Tests motif execution and image-over-explanation.
- Strengths: Pulse and near blade can be written as sensation and action.
- Defects: Motifs are episode-level only; preserve their placements in generation. Low, generation constraint.
- Reader impact: Body-based memory keeps the reveal from becoming a lore lecture.

#### Character Critic
- Stance: Tests profile-backed recognition, agency, and relationship pressure.
- Strengths: Baek’s external-haul profile supports the route; Jinwoo’s refusal to self-dose reveals protective agency; physician’s limits prevent omniscient diagnosis.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The old companion relationship becomes emotionally costly before motive is resolved.
