## Design Critique (required personas)

#### Target Reader
- Stance: Adult male web-novel reader seeking tactical evidence, family reversal, and a hard next-episode pull.
- Strengths: The ledger produces an immediate actionable lead; the empty cages deliver a concrete payoff; the paternity claim is a high-value hook.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The reader has a reason to continue because the accusation attacks both 도현 and 진우’s identity without resolving either.

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: 회귀 무협·문파 장악물 contract and escalation.
- Strengths: Prior knowledge creates the search advantage, while altered evidence prevents effortless omniscience; the 폐광 yields a tangible win and a larger threat.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Delivers investigation-driven 사이다 without replacing action with exposition.

#### Plot Expert
- Stance: Causality, hook alignment, and scope.
- Strengths: Each Transition causes the next Situation; the series Hook is repeated at equal strength in Out and Scene 3 Turn; no extra chase or faction reveal crowds the ending.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The closing question is clean: is the child’s claim true, planted, or strategically distorted?

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: Serialization, opening retention, and closing density.
- Strengths: The opener starts with a physical trace; Scene 2 turns travel into evidence; the closing has one independent obligation rather than a stack of reveals.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Low skim risk and a strong click-through reason for Episode 038.

#### Literary Critic
- Stance: Motif, sensory pressure, and restraint.
- Strengths: Blank records and empty cages make erasure visible; water drops carry time pressure; the design refuses to explain the seal or absolve 도현.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The family reversal gains weight through objects and bodies rather than thematic declaration.

#### Character Critic
- Stance: Motivation, voice, and profile-backed identity claims.
- Strengths: 진우’s evidence-first restraint fits his drive; 혁’s protection demand fits his 명분; the child’s 출구-counting behavior and repeated short voice are profiled; the paternity claim is clearly presented as the child’s testimony, not confirmed fact.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The reader can distrust the claim without distrusting the child, preserving both tension and empathy.

#### Setting/Lore Expert
- Stance: Location fidelity and world-rule restraint.
- Strengths: All location facets are approved anchors; the staging profiles lock blocking and props; seal/medicine mechanics are not expanded beyond the world-bible.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The setting feels materially traceable, supporting the serial mystery rather than becoming a lore lecture.
