## Design Critique (required personas)
#### Target Reader
- Stance: Reads for revenge action, family reversal, and immediate tactical stakes.
- Strengths: The first-ring payoff becomes a body-level crisis; the final absorption is a strong click-through hook.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The reader gets escalation rather than a pause after the duel.

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: Tests martial regression and revenge-family genre contracts.
- Strengths: Jinwoo makes an active but dangerous intervention; the villain weaponizes the expected rescue.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The episode preserves cathartic momentum while raising cost.

#### Plot Expert
- Stance: Tests causality and exact Hook strength/scope.
- Strengths: First-ring release causes poison flow; poison flow causes inference; confession attempt causes collapse; next-ring manipulation causes absorption.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The hook is earned by the prior episode rather than appended arbitrarily.

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: Tests serialization rhythm and Out density.
- Strengths: Three distinct scene functions; one dominant closing obligation; no crowded stack of separate revelations.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The episode is sellable as a complete crisis unit with a concrete next click.

#### Literary Critic
- Stance: Tests motif, restraint, and embodied meaning.
- Strengths: Open ring and black wave turn protection into shared injury without a thematic lecture.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The emotional meaning remains discoverable through physical movement.

#### Character Critic
- Stance: Tests motivation, voice, and profile-backed inference.
- Strengths: Jinwoo’s mistaken conclusion follows observed matching reactions; Dohyun’s failed confession follows his established contract silence.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: No premature forgiveness or arbitrary betrayal reverses the father-son conflict.
