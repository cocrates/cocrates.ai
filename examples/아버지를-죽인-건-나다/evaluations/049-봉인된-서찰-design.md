## Design Critique
#### Target Reader
- Stance: Wants a concrete family-reversal advancement, not a delayed letter with no event.
- Strengths: The letter is physically secured and immediately contested; the watcher’s line creates a sharp click-through.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The reader receives a tactical win and a more dangerous contradiction.

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: Tests revenge-wuxia promise and controlled reveal.
- Strengths: Jinwoo refuses to open a suspicious letter, while still forcing an enemy mistake.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The episode gives genre action without cashing the core mystery early.

#### Plot Expert
- Stance: Checks causality and Hook scope.
- Strengths: 048 coordinate → recovery → bait → watcher’s statement → suspicion is clean.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The closing question follows from behavior, not author assertion.

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: Checks serialization and closing density.
- Strengths: One dominant close; no competing mother reveal or letter-text dump.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Strong next-episode pull with low confusion risk.

#### Literary Critic
- Stance: Checks restraint and object-based meaning.
- Strengths: The unbroken seal carries desire, distrust, and control.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Emotional conflict remains embodied in whether Jinwoo opens the letter.

#### Character Critic
- Stance: Checks Jinwoo/Hyuk and the additive watcher profile.
- Strengths: Jinwoo prioritizes provenance; Hyuk prioritizes public proof; watcher prioritizes erasure.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Three distinct motives press on one object.

#### Setting/Lore Expert
- Stance: Checks the new cast profile and columbarium logic.
- Strengths: Watcher’s gear/state and fire threat are catalogued; the same facets and seal mechanics continue.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The watcher belongs to the established record network rather than appearing as a random villain.
