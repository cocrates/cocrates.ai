## Design Critique (required personas)
#### Target Reader
- Stance: Wants tactical pursuit, a meaningful rescue, and an immediate next target.
- Strengths: The cage question is answered with a character reversal, not a false rescue.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: High continuation pull from Gahwan plus the valley map.

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: Tests regression-action payoff and controlled escalation.
- Strengths: Jinwoo does not solve the route alone; Hyuk’s evidence wins the argument, preserving earned competence.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Power remains bounded and tactical.

#### Plot Expert
- Stance: Checks Hook alignment, causality, and scope.
- Strengths: Every clue creates the next motion; the Out claims only the valley direction.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Clear next objective without stealing Episode 088’s conflict.

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: Checks testimony density and closing sellability.
- Strengths: The rescue occurs before the map explanation; the last image is one dominant obligation.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Low skim risk and a concrete click-through hook.

#### Literary Critic
- Stance: Checks motifs, sensory pairing, and restraint.
- Strengths: Broken thread and empty cage evolve rather than repeat; the map closes on an image.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Family absence remains felt without melodrama.

#### Character Critic
- Stance: Checks motivation, voices, and profile-backed knowledge.
- Strengths: Gahwan’s map custody is now profile-grounded; his limited knowledge and habitual subject omission match his voice.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The new evidence feels earned rather than author-inserted.

#### Setting/Lore Expert
- Stance: Checks terrain anchors, logistics, and world rules.
- Strengths: The chase uses exact first-base and valley facets; decoy tracks have physical evidence.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The enemy’s mobile infrastructure becomes credible without a catalog lecture.
