## Design Critique (required personas)
#### Target Reader
- Stance: Adult male web-novel reader seeking revenge payoff, tactical action, and family reversal.
- Strengths: Immediate continuation from the kill declaration; the duel has a visible tactical objective; the seal-ring release is a strong serial pull.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The reader can enjoy an action payoff without spending the episode on a full explanation that would weaken the next hook.

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: Tests regression-martial-arts and revenge-family genre promises.
- Strengths: The protagonist makes an active tactical choice; the father’s contradictory blade behavior sustains the central trope beyond a flat villain reveal.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The genre contract is delivered through combat and reversal rather than delayed courtroom exposition.

#### Plot Expert
- Stance: Tests causal escalation, Hook body alignment, and Hook scope.
- Strengths: Declaration causes duel; duel moves the bloodline field; the Lord’s intervention causes the exact Out hook.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: No causal gap asks the reader to accept an arbitrary battle or an unrelated cliffhanger.

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: Tests serialization rhythm, scene handoffs, and closing density.
- Strengths: Three clean functions; the closing has one primary obligation and no stacked chase/reveal signals.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The episode reads as a complete action unit while leaving one urgent next-step question.

#### Literary Critic
- Stance: Tests motif, sensory restraint, and whether meaning is dramatized rather than announced.
- Strengths: The stopped blade and wet stone translate moral ambiguity into physical staging; the closing image avoids thematic explanation.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: Emotional complexity survives the action pace without alienating a reader who came for momentum.

#### Character Critic
- Stance: Tests profile-backed motivation, relationship pressure, and voice.
- Strengths: Dohyun’s recognition and contradictory blade are grounded in the established father-son relationship; Jinwoo chooses evidence through action rather than trusting memory.
- Defects: —
- Reader impact: The father-son conflict deepens without prematurely absolving Dohyun or reversing Jinwoo’s revenge drive.
