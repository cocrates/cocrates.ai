- Stance: Adult male serial reader seeking revenge escalation, family reversal, and concrete clues.
- Strengths: The apparent “another son” reveal is tested rather than accepted; the mark and internal handoff create a clickable next step.
- Defects: — (all assigned retention checks are negative; no crowded Out).
- Reader impact: The reader gets an earned partial answer and a stronger operational mystery without losing 도현 as a suspect.

#### Genre Critic
- Stance: Checks regression/martial-arts revenge promises and payoff timing.
- Strengths: Evidence-based reversal, faction logistics, and the protagonist’s refusal to claim a cheap bloodline victory.
- Defects: —.
- Reader impact: Satisfies readers who want plot movement rather than a tearful rescue pause.

#### Plot Expert
- Stance: Checks causality, Hook alignment, and Out scope.
- Strengths: Each scene’s Turn causes the next; Summary, Out, and closing Turn hold one evidence strength.
- Defects: — (Hook body and scope checked; no creep).

#### Reader-Editor
- Stance: Checks serialization clarity and closing sellability.

- Defects: — (Out density checked; only one dominant obligation).
